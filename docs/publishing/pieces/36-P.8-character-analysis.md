## The piece

- **Parity with:** ProWritingAid's character analysis.
- **The writer gets:** for each main character: which chapters they appear in,
  how much they speak, whether their way of speaking stays consistent, and
  (with a model) how they change across the book.
- **Uses:** `dialogue_craft.speech_profiles`, `dialogue_speakers`, the character
  entries, and `personality_assessor` if 0.1 finds it fits.
- **Where:** on the existing Characters screen, as a section on each character.
  No new tab.
- **Tiers:** presence, speech share and speech consistency need no model. The
  arc needs one.
- **Done when:** a character who vanishes for ten chapters, and one whose speech
  changes without cause, are both visible.
