## Phase context

No new screen in this phase except one small form. Its output is a report the
scorecard will display.

## The piece

- **Adds checks:** pacing against the genre, and promises and subplots touched.
- **Adds information, not a check:** chapter length and manuscript length beside
  what is typical. Shown on the scorecard without a colour and left out of
  the readiness figure.
- **Uses:** `publishing_goal` from 1.1, `pacing_analyzer.compare_to_genre`,
  `StoryPlanning.promises` and each chapter's subplot notes.
- **Book-level results:** promises or subplots that no chapter touches.
- **Tiers:** without the genre baseline data, the pacing check reports "no
  baseline" and does not count against readiness.
- **Done when:** the checks appear, with the book-level result and the length
  information.
