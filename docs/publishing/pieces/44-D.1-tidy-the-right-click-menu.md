## The piece

- **The writer gets:** a short menu. One **Improve this…** entry at the top
  opens help for the selection. Spelling and grammar fixes stay directly
  visible. Everything else is grouped under a few headings.
- **Read first:** the context-menu code in `src/ui/manuscript_editor.py` and
  `src/ui/enhanced_text_editor.py`.
- **Until D.2 is built,** "Improve this…" opens Suggestions, and Rephrase and
  "Overly descriptive?" sit beside it in a small group.
- **Done when:** the top level of the menu has no more than about ten entries
  and every old action is still reachable.

**Agreed in 0.2** (counts and findings in [screens.md](../screens.md)):

- **Today:** 6 top-level entries and 12 items without a selection; 11 and 22
  with one. The menu is `ChapterEditor._show_context_menu` in
  `src/ui/manuscript_editor.py`.
- **"Improve this…"** replaces the three entries Rephrase with AI, Suggestions
  and Overly descriptive?. Until D.2 is built it opens a small submenu of the
  three, so each is still one click away. AI Thesaurus and the Synonyms
  submenu stay as they are.
- **A flagged word's fixes at the top.** `EnhancedTextEditor._show_context_menu`
  already builds this section (Replace with, Ask, Suggest in context, Rewrite
  with AI, Ignore, Add to dictionary), but `ChapterEditor` disconnects it, so
  right-clicking a flag in the Write tab shows none of it today. Reuse that
  code; do not copy it (rule 14). The hover card stays exactly as it is.
- **Fix Cut and Copy.** They never appear today, because the selection is
  checked on the cursor made at the click position, not on the editor's
  cursor.
- **Fix the Checks menu tooltip,** which tells the writer to right-click a word
  for suggestions. After this piece that is true again.
- **Capability checklist:** every entry in today's menu (both trees are listed
  in screens.md), and where it is afterwards. Ticked before the piece is done.
  No entry is removed without its action being reachable.
- **Count:** the selection menu's top level goes from 11 to at most 9, before
  the flag section, which only shows on a flagged word.

## What has changed since this brief was written

_Added 24 November 2026, after phase 3, D.3 and 2.5 were built. Read the Outcome of each piece named here._

- **The flag section has more in it now.** `EnhancedTextEditor._show_context_menu`
  also adds a **"Fix all N like this"** tick box under "Replace with:" and
  "Fix all like this…" below the fixes (`src/ui/fix_all.py`, 3.4). Ticking
  the box leaves the menu open; the fix chosen next is made in every place.
- **These are not reachable in the Write tab today,** for the reason this
  brief gives: `ChapterEditor` disconnects that menu. The hover card has the
  box, so the feature works, but the right-click route only works once this
  piece reuses the flag section. Test it there.
- **Capability checklist additions:** the Fix-all box and list; the Write
  menu's Style checks submenu (`src/ui/style_checks_menu.py`, D.3).
- **Counts:** the Write menu is at 40 items against a baseline of 29. This
  piece is the place to bring it back down (for example, the submenu counted
  as one entry, or its switches grouped). Record the new figures in
  `screens_baseline.json` only when the writer agrees.
- **Added 24 November 2026: group the Style checks switches.** The Write
  menu's Style checks submenu (`src/ui/style_checks_menu.py`) lists every
  switch at one level, which took the Write menu from 29 items to 40. Group
  them under three headings, **Words**, **Sentences** and **Line**, each a
  submenu holding its switches, with "All on" and "All off" at the top.
  Every switch stays, keeps its stored setting and stays in step with the
  Style tab's boxes while that tab exists. The harness then counts a
  submenu's switches one level down; agree the count with the writer in
  [D.6](48b-D.6-retire-the-trial-screens.md) before recording a new baseline.
- **Updating:** the switches' stored values are not renamed or moved. A
  project saved before this piece shows the same checks on and off after it.

## Outcome

**Built 24 November 2026.**

**What the writer gets:** the Write tab's right-click menu.

- **On a flagged word, its fixes come first:**
  - Replace with, then the "Fix all N like this" box and "Fix all like this…";
  - Ask, Suggest in context, Rewrite with AI;
  - Ignore, Add to dictionary.

  This had been unreachable in the Write tab.
- **With a selection:**
  - **✨ Improve this ▸** holds Rephrase with AI, Suggestions and Overly descriptive? until D.2 merges them;
