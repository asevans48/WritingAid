## Phase context

**The idea.** Ratings stay in one shared store, so everything rated in any
project keeps teaching the app. Each rating is tagged with the voice of the
writing it was given to. Ratings fall into a small number of voice groups.
When the app writes a rewrite, it learns first from ratings in the same voice
group as the passage, whichever project they came from. Two books in different
voices stop colouring each other; two in a similar voice help each other; and
one book with several narrators gets a group for each.

**My reading of "broad, manageable".** Broad: shared across projects, with a
few large groups, not many small ones. Manageable: the writer can see the
groups, rename them, move a rating, merge two groups, and remove ratings.
Correct this in the review notes if you meant something else.

## The piece

- **Build:** when Suggestions, Rephrase, Expand or the Companion's fixes choose
  rated examples, work out the voice group of the passage being rewritten and
  prefer examples from that group. Within a group, this project's ratings
  still come first, as they do today. If the group has too few examples, the
  nearest group fills in.
- **Read first:** `examples_prompt` in `src/ai/rewrite_quality.py`,
  `rated_examples` in `src/data/rephrase_database.py`.
- **Prove it first:** on the benchmark and on sample chapters, compare the voice
  fit of rewrites made with same-group examples against today's choice. Keep
  the change only if voice fit is the same or better.
- **Backwards compatible:** with one group, the examples chosen are exactly
  today's.
- **Tests:** with two voice groups, a passage in one voice is never given the
  other voice's examples while its own group has enough.
- **Done when:** the comparison is written down and the examples follow the
  voice group.
