## The piece

- **The writer gets:** for mechanical fixes only, such as one misspelling
  repeated through a chapter, one button applies them all after showing the
  list.
- **Limits:** never offered for a prose rewrite. One undo step reverses all of
  them.
- **Done when:** a repeated misspelling is fixed in one action and undone in one.

## Outcome

**Status:** done. A repeated misspelling is fixed in one action and undone
in one (`tests/test_fix_all.py`).

**Built ahead of 3.2.** The next-fix queue doesn't exist yet, so "Fix all like
this…" sits where fixes are made today: the editor's hover card (a link
beside *Add to dictionary* and *Ignore*) and the right-click menu (below
*Replace with:*). It appears only when the flag is mechanical and the flagged
words occur at least twice in the chapter. When 3.2 is built, its fix view
can call `fix_all.offer(editor, error)`.

**New module `src/ui/fix_all.py`:**

- `is_mechanical(error)`: spelling, or a grammar rule in
  `readiness_judge.SURE_RULES` (doubled word, space before or after
  punctuation, double space, lowercase "i'm"). Never style, overuse,
  repeated phrases, confused words (their/there can be right in the next
  sentence) or the model's corrections. A suggestion over three words
  (`MAX_WORDS`) is a rewrite and never offered.
- `places(editor, error, suggestion)` finds every place with the same flag
  using the editor's live rules (`FlagState.from_highlighter`), so ignored
  words and dictionary words are left alone. Only paragraphs that contain
  the words are checked. Spelling keeps the writer's case
  (`_match_word_case`, imported from the editor, not copied); a grammar
  fix uses each place's own suggestion ("The the" → "The").
- `apply(editor, places)` makes every change in one edit block, so one undo
  reverses them all. A place whose text changed after the list was shown
  is skipped.
- `FixAllDialog` lists each place in its sentence (`[teh → the]`), each
  with a tick box. Spelling with several suggestions gets a *Replace with*
  choice. The button reads *Fix all N* or *Fix N*.
- `offer`, `add_to_card` and `add_to_menu`: the two call lines in
  `enhanced_text_editor.py` (rule 14).

**Counts:** the Write tab is unchanged (buttons 24, menu items 29, right-click
12 and 22). The new link and menu entry appear only on a repeated mechanical
flag. Screenshots of the dialog at 1024 × 600, 1440 × 900, 2560 × 1440 and
150% were taken by hand; the harness has no screen for the card or this
dialog. The Write tab's 150% minimum-size warning was there before this
piece.

**Left out:** fixing across chapters (the brief says "through a chapter";
other chapters aren't open in the editor, and one undo couldn't reverse
them), and a "Fix all" from the Critique's grouped items (×N). That belongs
with 3.2's queue.

**Changed after review (2026-11-24).** The writer couldn't choose which fix
went everywhere: picking a fix on the card fixed one place and closed it.
Now:

- **A "Fix all N like this" box**, on the hover card (above the fix buttons)
  and in the right-click menu (under "Replace with:"). Ticking it leaves the
  card or menu open. The fix picked next is made in all N places, as one
  undo, and the status bar says how many. Unticked, a fix is made in one
  place, as before.
- **Every kind of flag**, at the writer's request: spelling, grammar,
  overused words and style. The box is opt-in, and "See all N…" still lets
  the writer leave some. Fixes longer than six words are rewrites and aren't
  offered.
- **The list gets a fix picker for every kind**, not just spelling.
- **Kept as shortcuts:** the list dialog, as "See all N…" on the card and
  "Fix all like this…" in the menu.
- **Code:** `fix_all.everywhere`, called first in the editor's `_apply_fix`;
  `card_hidden`, called in `ErrorCard.hideEvent` (call lines only).
