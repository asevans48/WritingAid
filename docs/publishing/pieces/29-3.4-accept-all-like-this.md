## The piece

- **The writer gets:** for mechanical fixes only, such as one misspelling
  repeated through a chapter, one button applies them all after showing the
  list.
- **Limits:** never offered for a prose rewrite. One undo step reverses all of
  them.
- **Done when:** a repeated misspelling is fixed in one action and undone in one.

## Review notes

_The writer's changes to this brief, made before the session starts._

## When you finish

- The piece's own tests pass, then the full suite once.
- Screenshots from the harness at 1024 × 600, 1440 × 900, 2560 × 1440 and 150% scaling (by hand until U.2 exists).
- Fill in *Outcome* below and tick this piece in the [tracker](../README.md#tracker).
- Nothing is committed. Do not start the next piece.

## Outcome

_Filled in by the session: what was built, the files and function names added
or changed, anything left out and why, and anything the next piece should know._
