## The piece

- **Why:** phase 3, D.3 and 2.5 built their replacements **alongside** the
  old screens, as the rules require. Until the old ones go, the app has
  more on screen, not less. This is the "separate small step" those pieces
  promised.
- **Before starting, the writer has** used the next-fix queue, the Write
  menu's Style checks and the Critique's own window on both projects, and
  listed under *Review notes* which of the items below may go.
- **What is on trial, and what happens to each** (nothing is cut: each
  action keeps a home and each old entry stays as a shortcut, decision 10):

  | On trial | Replaced by | When retired |
  |---|---|---|
  | The old "Today's three fixes" list, behind a link in the Next fixes section | The next-fix card (3.2) | The link goes; "From the scorecard" fixes (2.2) show on the card |
  | The Style tab in the side panel (15 buttons) | The queue's line stage and Write ▸ Style checks (D.3) | The tab goes, with a one-time note saying where its parts went; "Style issues panel…" stays in the menu as a shortcut to the queue |
  | Any other section the D.3 and 3.2 Outcomes list as "on trial" | As listed there | As listed there |

- **Bring the counts back under the baseline** (rule 13). Today, against
  `screens_baseline.json`:
  - Companion: 15 buttons (baseline 18), 7 sections (7). Fine.
  - Write menu: 40 items (baseline 29). The rise is the Style checks
    submenu's switches. Either the harness counts a submenu as one entry
    (if the writer agrees that is the honest count), or the switches are
    grouped. Coordinate with [D.1](44-D.1-tidy-the-right-click-menu.md).
  - Critique tab: one button more ("⧉ Own window", 2.5).
  Record the new baseline only when the writer agrees to it.
- **Capability checklist first:** everything each retired screen could do,
  and where it is now. Ticked before anything is removed.
- **Backwards compatible:** settings that remember a retired section's state
  are left in place and ignored. A saved layout naming the Style tab opens
  without it and without an error.
- **Done when:** the items the writer listed are gone, every action on the
  checklist is reachable, and the harness counts are at or under the
  baseline.

  - The side panel goes from 4 tabs to 3 when the Style tab is retired. A
    saved panel state that names the Style tab's position must open on the
    Companion's queue, not on the wrong tab and not with an error. Test it

  - The Style checks grouping is in [D.1](44-D.1-tidy-the-right-click-menu.md);
    the toolbar, hover card and Companion sections are in

    its settings as it left them: nothing a retired screen stored is deleted.


## Build notes

_Added 24 November 2026 so a fresh session can finish this piece without searching. Names were checked against the code on that date; if one has moved, find it with a search and say so in Outcome._

- **Read first:** `install` in `src/ui/next_fixes.py` (the "Today's three
  fixes ▸" link and `panel.older_fixes`); `set_fixes` in
  `src/ui/companion_panel.py`; `_show_right_panel` and the side panel's tab
  order in `src/ui/manuscript_editor.py` (the harness's `PANEL_INDEX` in
  `scripts/screen_harness.py` names the indexes); `src/ui/style_checks_menu.py`.
- **Only retire what the writer listed** under *Review notes*. If that list
  is empty, do nothing but the counts and say so.
- **The Style tab's index.** Other code opens panel tabs by number. Removing
  a tab shifts the ones after it. Find every `_show_right_panel(` and
  `right_panel.setCurrentIndex(` call and every saved index in settings;
  map old numbers to the new ones in one function. Test with each old value.
- **Capability checklist for the Style tab:** every one of its 15 buttons and
  each list action (jump to the issue, rewrite, ignore, switch a check on or
  off, refresh), and where each is in the queue or the menu.
- **Tests:** a settings file saved before the piece, naming the Style tab,
  opens on the Companion; every checklist action is reachable; harness
  counts at or under the baseline.
- **Stop and report if** any Style tab action has no home yet. Do not
  retire the tab in that case.
