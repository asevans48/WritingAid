## Phase context

Phase F makes the app work for stories that are not novels, **keeping every
story feature**: plot, characters, world, critique, the Improve window, Read
Aloud, the Studio. Agreed with the writer on 26 November 2026 (README
decision 18):

- **Kinds.** A project has a kind, chosen when it is created and changeable
  later: **Novel** (novels and novellas, as today, and the default for every
  existing project), **Video series**, **Podcast**, **Editorial series**,
  **Journalism**.
- **Same shape for every kind.** A project still holds a main manuscript,
  novellas and chapters. A kind changes the words on screen (set by the
  kind, editable per project), which checks and goals apply, and what
  publishing prepares. A chapter is one video, one episode or one part.
- **Nothing a novelist has changes.** A Novel project looks and behaves
  exactly as it does before this phase (rule 11). Every test of today's
  behaviour keeps passing unchanged.
- **Phase F comes after the final sweep (9.1).** Sending anything to a site
  (YouTube and others) stays a later integration (decision 7); this phase
  prepares the material.

## The piece

Write the design the other phase F pieces build from. **No code.** The
writer reviews it before F.1 starts (as with D.2a and 8.1a).

- **1. The kinds and their words.** A table: for each kind, the word for
  the project, the main manuscript, a novella, a chapter, and the unit of
  length. Starting point, to confirm or correct:

  | Kind | Novella | Chapter | Length |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Novel | Novella | Chapter | words |
  | Video series | Series | Video | minutes |
  | Podcast | Season | Episode | minutes |
  | Editorial series | Series | Part | words |
  | Journalism | Series | Article | words |

  The writer can rename each word per project. Say where the words are
  kept and how every screen gets them from one place.
- **2. What a chapter is in each kind.** Agreed: a chapter is one video,
  one episode or one part, and a novella is a story told over several of
  them. A single retelling (one video) is a project with one chapter, or
  one chapter of the main manuscript among others. Say how sections inside
  one video or episode are marked (headings, which become timestamps).
- **3. What each kind switches on and off.** One table of every screen,
  check, goal and export in the app (from `inventory.md` and
  `screens.md`), with a column per kind: as today, renamed, replaced, or
  off. Nothing is deleted for any kind; "off" means hidden for that kind
  and its data kept.
- **4. Where the kind is stored.** A field on the project that older
  versions of the app ignore, absent meaning Novel. What kind-only data
  (sources, cues, runtime targets, video links) is stored where, so a
  Novel project's file does not change at all.
- **5. Changing kind later.** Nothing is lost: data the new kind does not
  show is kept and comes back if the kind is changed back.
- **6. Choosing a kind.** Where the choice sits when a project is created
  and where it is changed, with no new top-level control (rule 13).
- **7. The order of the build pieces** F.1 to F.15, what each needs from
  the others, and which need a model (rules 1, 2 and 9: every feature
  works without a model; a model only adds to it).
- **8. Mock-ups** of the Write screen and the tree (D.10) for a Video
  series and a Journalism project at 1024 × 600 and 1440 × 900.
- **9. Open questions for the writer**, each with a recommendation.

## Already answered by the writer (26 November 2026)

- A video stands for a **chapter** in a novel, novella or story, and for
  an **episode** in a video series or podcast. One video is never split
  over several chapters.

- A kind is chosen per project (not per manuscript).
- The words are set by the kind and editable.
- Formats covered: video story scripts, podcasts, editorial series,
  journalism, and novels and novellas as they are today.
- An uploaded video goes to the Studio for the selected chapter and
  becomes a card that can be refined there (F.10).
- Spoken formats gain: a runtime estimate, hook and retention checks,
  checks written for the ear, speakers and cues.
- Nonfiction gains: a sources list, an unsourced-claim check, real people
  and a timeline, and fiction checks off.
- Publishing prepares: a video package, a podcast package, an article
  export, a series page, and the novel or novella export, including a
  compilation of a project with its novellas and a single novella on its
  own as a novel, story or short story.
