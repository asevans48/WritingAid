## The piece

- **Also counts:** for each screen, the tabs, sections, menu items and visible
  buttons, so rule 13 can be checked against the 0.2 baseline.
- **Why:** checking a screen by hand at several sizes is slow and costs a
  session's attention every time. One command should do it.
- **Build:** a script that opens a named screen on a sample project, renders it
  at 1024 × 600, 1440 × 900 and 2560 × 1440 and once at 150% scaling, and
  saves the images to a scratch folder. It also reports any widget drawn
  smaller than a readable height and any window larger than its screen.
- **Done when:** one command produces the images for Suggestions, the Companion
  and the style panel.
