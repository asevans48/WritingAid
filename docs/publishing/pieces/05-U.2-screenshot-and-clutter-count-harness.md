## The piece

- **Also counts:** for each screen, the tabs, sections, menu items and visible
  buttons, so rule 13 can be checked against the 0.2 baseline.
- **Why:** checking a screen by hand at several sizes is slow and costs a
  session's attention every time. One command should do it.
- **Build:** a script that opens a named screen on a sample project, renders it
  at 1024 × 600, 1440 × 900 and 2560 × 1440 and once at 150% scaling, and
  saves the images to a scratch folder. It also reports any widget drawn
  smaller than a readable height and any window larger than its screen.
- **Done when:** one command produces the images for Suggestions, the Companion
  and the style panel.

## Outcome

**Status:** done.

**One command:**

    venv/bin/python scripts/screen_harness.py                       # all 17 screens
    venv/bin/python scripts/screen_harness.py suggestions companion style
    venv/bin/python scripts/screen_harness.py --list
    venv/bin/python scripts/screen_harness.py --compare <earlier run folder>

It draws each screen at 1024 × 600, 1440 × 900, 2560 × 1440, and 1440 × 900
at 150%. Each size runs in its own process with a **simulated screen of that
size**, so "opens no larger than the screen" is tested for real. The 150%
run uses `QT_SCALE_FACTOR`. All 17 screens at all four sizes take about 10
seconds. Images and `report.json` go to a scratch folder, whose path is
printed. The screens are drawn on a small sample project built in code,
with `HOME` pointing at a throwaway folder, the companion, critique-history
and window-position stores redirected, and LanguageTool and models off.
Nothing real is touched.

**It reports:**

- **pages and windows that do not fit the screen:** a tab or panel whose
  smallest size is wider or taller than the screen, or a window that opens
  larger than it;
- **text squeezed below a readable height:** a one-line widget smaller than
  one line of its own font (or its own minimum, for small print), or a list
  or text area under two lines;
- **counts:** visible buttons, items behind menu buttons, sections and
  inner tabs for each screen, plus both right-click menus. They are taken at
  1440 × 900 on the same sample project as 0.2, and compared with
  `docs/publishing/screens_baseline.json`. Any rise is a rule 13 problem;
- **`--compare`:** each image against an earlier run, pixel for pixel. This
  is the proof that a refactor changed nothing visible. Two runs back to
  back give 68 of 68 identical images.

Exit code 0 means no problems; 1 means at least one.

**Files:**

- `scripts/screen_harness.py`: `run(names, out, sizes)`,
  `compare_counts(counts, baseline)`, `compare_images(old, new)`,
  `count_widget(widget)`, and `SCREENS`, the 17 screens it knows.
- `docs/publishing/screens_baseline.json`: the 0.2 counts, readable by
  the script. They are the same numbers as the table in `screens.md`.
- `tests/test_screen_harness.py`: 4 tests, covering a real run of
  Suggestions, the Companion and the Style panel, the counts matching the
  baseline, a reported rise, and the image comparison.
