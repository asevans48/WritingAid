## The piece

- **Prove it first:** run the tension rating twice on the same chapters. If the
  two runs disagree often, show the scene summaries and leave tension out.
- **Adds:** for each scene, what happens, whose point of view it is, what the
  scene is for, and a tension rating. One call per chapter, cached with the
  chapter hash.
- **Also adds two readiness checks:** scenes with no clear purpose, and point of
  view slips. These fill gaps noted in 0.1.
- **Done when:** the outline shows the model's reading and the two checks appear
  on the scorecard.
