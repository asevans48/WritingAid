## Phase context

Piece 2.1 is built straight after 1.2, with only two checks to show, so that
each later check appears on screen as it is added.

## The piece

- **The writer gets:** the readiness figure with its change since the last
  check, and a small trend for the book.
- **Uses:** `src/data/critique_history.py` if its shape fits, otherwise a short
  history kept in `readiness_state`.
- **Done when:** two checks on different days show a change.

## Outcome

**Status:** done, 23 November 2026. It fills in over days.

**Built:**
- **The record:** `readiness_state.history` and `record_history`, one entry
  per manuscript per day (the latest check of the day wins, a year kept). It
  is stored in `readiness.json` under the manuscript's `history` with the
  figure, `FIGURE_VERSION`, `THRESHOLDS_VERSION` and the tier.
  `critique_history.py` was not used: its shape is per critique run, not per
  readiness figure.
- **Next to the figure:** "▲ 6 since 21 Nov", from
  `readiness.change_since_last`, against the last check on an earlier day.
  Nothing is shown when the formula or thresholds changed in between, so a
  new formula never looks like progress.
- **A small trend:** the last 30 days as a sparkline, with the daily figures
  in its tooltip.

**Tests:** `tests/test_readiness_history.py` (4). Two checks on different
days show a change; one entry a day; no false change after a formula
change.
