## The piece

- **The writer gets:** fewer false alarms. Candidates from the scans are judged,
  as Suggestions does for overly descriptive statements.
- **Build:** `chapter_report(..., llm=...)`. At most one model call per stage per
  chapter, batching that stage's candidates. Results are cached with the
  chapter hash. Items the model clears are kept as "cleared" and not shown as
  problems.
- **Read first:** `review_chapter` in `src/ai/overdescription.py`,
  `src/ai/flag_judge.py`.
- **Tiers:** compact prompt for local models; a failed or unreadable reply falls
  back to the scan result.
- **Tests:** fake model clears one candidate and confirms another; an empty
  reply leaves the scan result in place.
- **Done when:** a report made with a model is marked as judged and differs from
  the scan only where the model decided.
