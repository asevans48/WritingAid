## Phase context

No new screen in this phase except one small form. Its output is a report the
scorecard will display.

## The piece

- **The writer gets:** fewer false alarms. Candidates from the scans are judged,
  as Suggestions does for overly descriptive statements.
- **Build:** `chapter_report(..., llm=...)`. At most one model call per stage per
  chapter, batching that stage's candidates. Results are cached with the
  chapter hash. Items the model clears are kept as "cleared" and not shown as
  problems.
- **Read first:** `review_chapter` in `src/ai/overdescription.py`,
  `src/ai/flag_judge.py`.
- **Tiers:** compact prompt for local models; a failed or unreadable reply falls
  back to the scan result.
- **Tests:** fake model clears one candidate and confirms another; an empty
  reply leaves the scan result in place.
- **Done when:** a report made with a model is marked as judged and differs from
  the scan only where the model decided.

## Outcome

**Status:** done, 23 November 2026. Tested with a stand-in model; the writer
tries it with a real one.

**Built:**
- `src/ai/readiness_judge.py`. With a model, each stage's candidates in a
  chapter go to it in **one request** (up to 40 items; 20 for a local model,
  with a shorter prompt), each with its sentence. The writer's stored voice
  reading (`voice_profile`) is part of the prompt, so their deliberate habits
  are let through.
  - **REAL** confirms an item.
  - **FINE** marks it `cleared`, with the model's reason (`Item.note`). It
    stays visible, listed under "Cleared by the model", and stops counting.
  - A model that clears nearly everything (90% of 8 or more) isn't trusted;
    the scan stands.
- In the engine, `chapter_report(..., llm=)`:
  - Judged results are cached under their own key and **preferred over the
    scan**, so the automatic scan never undoes them.
  - A chapter counts as judged only when **every** stage was. A failed or
    unreadable reply leaves that stage's scan, and it is tried again next
    time.
- **New check flags:**
  - `judge` (off for measures: pacing, sentence variety, chapter length).
  - `model_ready` (off where a model can't settle it here yet: Promises kept
    and Continuity in this book stay "needs a model", and say which Critique
    report covers them).
- With a model, Repeated words and Overly descriptive count, and Continuity
  across novellas uses `ContinuityAnalyzer` with the model.
- `BookReport.tier` is `scan`, `model` or `mixed`; the scorecard's note says
  which.

**Tests:** `tests/test_readiness_judge.py` (10). They cover:
- one candidate cleared and one confirmed;
- an empty or failed reply keeps the scan;
- caching, and the scan keeping verdicts;
- one request per stage;
- the voice reading in the prompt;
- the lazy-model guard;
- the local cap.
