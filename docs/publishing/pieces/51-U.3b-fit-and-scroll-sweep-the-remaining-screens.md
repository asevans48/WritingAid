## The piece

- **This session:** every window and dialog not covered in U.3a, a group at a
  time. If the list is long, do the worst offenders the harness reports and
  list the rest in *Outcome* for another session.
- **Do:** run the harness over the app's existing windows and dialogs, a group
  at a time. Fix what fails rule 12: add scrolling where content is squeezed
  or cut off, open windows within the screen, remove fixed heights on text.
- **Order:** the screens a writer uses most first: the manuscript editor and its
  side panel, Suggestions, Rephrase, the critique tab, the style panel,
  Settings. Then the rest.
- **Limit:** layout only. No change to what a screen does.
- **Done when:** the group passes the harness at all sizes. Repeat per group.

**What was wrong, and the fix**

- *Video Studio, 1,407 px (and so the whole main window):* its two toolbar
  rows were one line each, 1,375 px of chapter box, style box, buttons and
  a 622 px backends line. Both rows are `FlowLayout`s now: they wrap, a
  divider is left out where a row wraps, "Chapter:" and "Style:" stay with
  their boxes, and 🎤 Slide editor, 📑 Export deck and ✨ AI refine switch
  to 🎤 Slides, 📑 Export and ✨ Refine before the row wraps. The backends
  line and the status line elide (`ElidedLabel`); the backends line's tip
  shows the whole line above its own help. With the window narrower, the
  side panel's words (Backend status, Hints) ran over each other: the panel
  scrolls now. At 1,440 px and up the toolbar reads exactly as before.
- *World, 545 px high:* the section box (`QStackedWidget`) was as tall as
  its tallest section even when hidden (History & Timeline, 434 px). Each
  section is in a scroll area now (`scrollable`), so a short window scrolls
  the section shown. The subtitle wraps. At the tiled width the shared
  search, filter and sort row (`FilterSortWidget`, used by 12 sections) and
  the Factions buttons wrap instead of scrolling sideways.
- *Characters and Visuals at the tiled width:* their pages could not scroll
  sideways (a 400 px image preview; a character's form beside a 250 px
  list), so the right edge was cut. They scroll sideways now (one line
  each), as U.3a did for the Critique.
- *Windows taller than 600 px:* the World editors, the import guide and
  importer, the launcher, the Model Hub and two Training Studio dialogs.
  `ui_kit.scroll_dialog` puts a built window's contents in a scroll area,
  keeps its closing row (Save, Cancel, Close, "Export for: ▾ Export
  Prompts") below it, and opens it within the screen. One call line each.
- *The writer's narrow-rows rule (26 Nov):* `ui_kit.short_label(button,
  "short words")` gives a button (or a check box or label) in a
  `FlowLayout` row its narrow words. The row switches to them only when its
  full words do not fit on one line, and wraps only if the short ones still
  do not; with room again it reads as before. While short, the full name
  heads the tooltip (above any tip of its own). `ui_kit.full_text` reads a
  button's full words. Applied to U.3a's rows: the Companion's notes
  (💡 Suggest fix → 💡 Fix, Mark beat done → ✓ Beat, Dismiss → ✕; Go to
  and 💬 Ask unchanged), the rating bar ("Rate this …:" → "Rate:",
  ⭐ Excellent → ⭐ Best, Save for training → Save) and World's hierarchies
  (➕ Add Hierarchy → ➕ Add, so 📥 Import no longer wraps under it). The
  scorecard legend is a detail and still wraps. No new icon-only button
  apart from Dismiss → ✕, which the writer named.
- *The Critique's verdict (U.3a's screen, found here at 150 %):* its words
  sat beside "Ready, as far as checked" in a column 130 px wide and taller
  than the row, so their top was cut. In a window narrower than the verdict
  plus 360 px the words go under it (`ui_kit.stack_when_narrow`); wider, it
  looks as before.
- *Model Hub's ☆ rating:* each star's style was reset after it was made
  narrow, so the app's padding left a 28 px button too narrow for its star.
  The style sets no side padding now; the stars are 28 px as meant.
