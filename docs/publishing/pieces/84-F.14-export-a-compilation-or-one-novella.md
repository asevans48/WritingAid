## Phase context

Phase F makes the app work for stories that are not novels, **keeping every
story feature**: plot, characters, world, critique, the Improve window, Read
Aloud, the Studio. Agreed with the writer on 26 November 2026 (README
decision 18):

- **Kinds.** A project has a kind, chosen when it is created and changeable
  later: **Novel** (novels and novellas, as today, and the default for every
  existing project), **Video series**, **Podcast**, **Editorial series**,
  **Journalism**.
- **Same shape for every kind.** A project still holds a main manuscript,
  novellas and chapters. A kind changes the words on screen (set by the
  kind, editable per project), which checks and goals apply, and what
  publishing prepares. A chapter is one video, one episode or one part.
- **Nothing a novelist has changes.** A Novel project looks and behaves
  exactly as it does before this phase (rule 11). Every test of today's
  behaviour keeps passing unchanged.
- **Phase F comes after the final sweep (9.1).** Sending anything to a site
  (YouTube and others) stays a later integration (decision 7); this phase
  prepares the material.

## The piece

The writer, 26 November 2026: the novel or novella export stays, with "a
compilation if outputting the project with novellas", and "users should
still be able to export an individual novella as a novel/story/short
story".

- **Find out first** what the export does today for a project with
  novellas (9.2's *Outcome* and the export code): which manuscript is
  exported, and what the title page says ("A Novel by …", "A Novella by
  …"). Put it in *Outcome* before changing anything.
- **The writer gets**, in the export that exists:
  - **What to export:** the open manuscript (as today), one chosen
    novella, or **the whole project as a compilation** (the main
    manuscript and the novellas the writer ticks, in an order they can
    change, each starting on its own title page or part page, with one
    table of contents).
  - **What to call it** on the title page: Novel, Novella, Story, Short
    story, Collection, or the writer's own word, with a suggestion from
    the word count. Remembered per manuscript.
  - For other kinds the same choice with the kind's words (a season, a
    series).
- Works for every format the export has today; the export-and-import
  check (9.2) covers the compilation.
- No chapter is changed by an export.
