  problem's items in the Companion, with the first one selected in the text.

## Outcome

**Status:** done, 23 November 2026.

**Built:** `src/ui/readiness_fixes.py`.
- **Double-click a scorecard cell**, or click **Work through these in the
  Companion →** above a check's findings. The chapter opens in Write through
  the normal chapter switch, the Companion lists the check's findings as
  fixes, and the first one is selected in the text.
  - Each fix offers the editor's own suggestion as a ready quick fix (the
    sentence with the word replaced), plus 💡 More options… for model
    rewrites.
  - Accept goes through the Companion's existing path (`_replace_span`).
- A click still shows the details in the scorecard, as the writer asked.
  Double-clicking a chapter's **name** still opens its detailed critique.
- For the whole-book and opening rows, it opens the chapter of the first
  finding.

**Tests:** `tests/test_readiness_fixes.py`. They cover:
- findings becoming fixes;
- one click to the sentence, then Accept;
- unsaved edits surviving the switch;
- double-click on a cell.
