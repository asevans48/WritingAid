## Phase context

Phase F makes the app work for stories that are not novels, **keeping every
story feature**: plot, characters, world, critique, the Improve window, Read
Aloud, the Studio. Agreed with the writer on 26 November 2026 (README
decision 18):

- **Kinds.** A project has a kind, chosen when it is created and changeable
  later: **Novel** (novels and novellas, as today, and the default for every
  existing project), **Video series**, **Podcast**, **Editorial series**,
  **Journalism**.
- **Same shape for every kind.** A project still holds a main manuscript,
  novellas and chapters. A kind changes the words on screen (set by the
  kind, editable per project), which checks and goals apply, and what
  publishing prepares. A chapter is one video, one episode or one part.
- **Nothing a novelist has changes.** A Novel project looks and behaves
  exactly as it does before this phase (rule 11). Every test of today's
  behaviour keeps passing unchanged.
- **Phase F comes after the final sweep (9.1).** Sending anything to a site
  (YouTube and others) stays a later integration (decision 7); this phase
  prepares the material.

## The piece

- **Why:** every later piece asks "what kind of project is this?" and
  "what is a chapter called here?". Both need one answer in one place.
- **The writer gets:**
  - A kind for each project: chosen when the project is created, changed
    in the project's settings. Existing projects are Novel, with no
    question asked and no change to their files.
  - The words on screen follow the kind: Chapter, Novella, Manuscript and
    "words" become the kind's words everywhere they appear (the tree,
    menus, tooltips, messages, the status bar, exports, the Companion's
    cards, the Critique).
  - Each word can be renamed per project (one small list in the project's
    settings, with Reset).
- **How:** one new module holds the kinds and the words
  (`src/models/project_kind.py` or as F.0 decides) with functions such as
  `chapter_word(project)`. Screens call it; no screen keeps its own copy
  of a word. Files over 3,000 lines get only call lines (rule 14).
- **Find every fixed word.** Search `src/ui` and the exports for
  "chapter", "novella" and "manuscript" in text shown to the writer. Put
  the list in *Outcome*, with each one changed or the reason it stays.
- **Not in this piece:** checks, goals and exports by kind (F.2 onward).
  A new kind behaves like Novel with other words until those pieces land.

## Tests

- A project file with no kind opens as Novel and saves byte for byte
  unchanged.
- Each kind shows its words on the Write screen, the tree, the chapter
  menu and one export; a renamed word shows everywhere and survives a
  save and reopen.
- Changing the kind and changing it back loses nothing.
- No fixed "Chapter" or "Novella" is left in the screens listed in
  *Outcome* (a test greps the list).
