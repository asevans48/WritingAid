### What changed in the phase 1 pieces

- **1.3:** sentence variety is not in `style_checks`; it comes from
  `ProWritingAnalyzer` (`sentence_length_score`). Overused words and
  phrases come from `OveruseDetector`; `repetition_judge` only gives the
  model's verdict on them. Filler words are in the `intensifier` kind.
- **1.4:** `ConsistencyChecker` takes `[(id, title, text)]` for one
  manuscript and takes 0.4 s for 20 chapters, so run it once per manuscript.
- **1.5:** pacing against genre uses the Critique's built-in
  `GENRE_PROFILES` (via `resolve_genre_profile`), which needs no download.
  The brief's `pacing_analyzer.compare_to_genre` needs the CONLIT data,
  which is missing in a fresh install, so the "no baseline" case would have
  been the usual one. Subplots touched can be computed exactly; **promises
  touched need a model,** because nothing links a chapter to a promise.
- **1.6:** `continuity_analysis` only compares **novellas with each other**,
  and `event_order` only checks **rewrites**. So continuity within one book
  needs a model (the Canon, Characters and Worldbuilding reports), and
  **order of events has no function to connect.** 1.6 now asks for a
  decision in its review notes: drop it as a gap, or build a labelled scan
  from the chapter and plot events.

### Also confirmed

- **Manuscripts:** use `project.iter_manuscripts()` →
  `[(scope, Manuscript)]`, main first. `project.manuscript` is the
  *active* manuscript, which may be a novella, so it must not be used to
  mean "the main one". Every chapter's text is in memory once the project
  opens. The open chapter's live text can be newer;
  `MainWindow._chapters_for_links()` handles that.
- **Editor flags for a closed chapter:** no plain function gives them; they
  live in `WritingHighlighter._errors`. A highlighter on an offscreen
  `QTextDocument` works without a window: 0.36 s a chapter, with spelling,
  overuse, repeated phrases and style. LanguageTool's flags arrive later,
  from its own worker. **For 1.2a to decide:** use that route, or call
  `style_checks`, `OveruseDetector`, `proofing` and `SpellChecker`
  directly (no Qt). Also decide whether readiness counts must match the
  editor's exactly.
- **Every Critique report's numbers need no model.** Each analyser's
  `_compute` step needs none; only the narrative does.

### Gaps

The expected gaps are confirmed: **scene purpose, point of view, whole-book
voice drift.** Also missing:

- order of events across a chapter or the book;
- continuity within one manuscript, without a model;
- a link from chapters to promises;
- sentence variety in `style_checks`;
- scans for transitions, pronouns, alliteration, homonyms, acronyms and
  sensory detail;
- pacing against genre from real books without the CONLIT download (the
  built-in ranges cover it until then).
