## Phase context

No new screen in this phase except one small form. Its output is a report the
scorecard will display.

## The piece

- **Why:** agents and editors report deciding within the first five to ten
  pages (see [what "publishable" means](../background.md#what-publishable-means-here)).
- **The writer gets:** the first chapter of each manuscript is held to a
  stricter standard and shown as its own row at the top of the scorecard.
- **Checks:** every line and copyedit check at a tighter threshold; how far in
  the first event of the plot arrives; how much of the opening is background
  before anything happens.
- **Tiers:** the thresholds and the position of the first dialogue or action
  need no model. Judging the hook and the inciting incident needs one.
- **Route:** stricter for agent submission.
- **Done when:** the opening row appears for the main manuscript and for each
  novella.
