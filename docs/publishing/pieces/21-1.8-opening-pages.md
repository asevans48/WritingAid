## Phase context

No new screen in this phase except one small form. Its output is a report the
scorecard will display.

## The piece

- **Why:** agents and editors report deciding within the first five to ten
  pages (see [what "publishable" means](../background.md#what-publishable-means-here)).
- **The writer gets:** the first chapter of each manuscript is held to a
  stricter standard and shown as its own row at the top of the scorecard.
- **Checks:** every line and copyedit check at a tighter threshold; how far in
  the first event of the plot arrives; how much of the opening is background
  before anything happens.
- **Tiers:** the thresholds and the position of the first dialogue or action
  need no model. Judging the hook and the inciting incident needs one.
- **Route:** stricter for agent submission.
- **Done when:** the opening row appears for the main manuscript and for each
  novella.

## Outcome

**Status:** done, 23 November 2026.

**Built:**
- **Opening pages row** at the top of the scorecard, for the main manuscript
  and each novella: the first ~2,500 words (about ten pages), across chapters
  when the first is short (`OpeningContext`, `level="opening"`). Its checks
  (`src/ai/readiness_checks/opening.py`):
  - **Words before something happens:** the first dialogue or action, found
    by a scan. Agent route: ready by 300 words, a problem past 800; self:
    500 / 1,200.
  - **Backstory up front:** sentences looking back (past perfect, "years
    ago", "used to") before anything happens. Agent: ready under 15%, a
    problem over 30%; self: 20% / 40%.
  - **First plot event:** where the plan's first event appears, found by its
    rarest words. A labelled scan, since nothing links plan to prose. It
    doesn't apply without plan events.
  - **Hook and inciting incident:** a model reads the opening once and
    answers both. Without a model it is "needs a model"; an unreadable answer
    is "could not check".
- **Chapter 1 is held to a stricter bar:** its line and copyedit thresholds
  are 0.6× on the agent route and 0.8× self-publishing, all in
  `CheckContext.threshold`.
- Route-dependent thresholds live in `_ROUTE_THRESHOLDS`. `THRESHOLDS_VERSION`
  is now 3.

**Tests:** `tests/test_opening_pages.py` (7).
