## Phase context

Phase F makes the app work for stories that are not novels, **keeping every
story feature**: plot, characters, world, critique, the Improve window, Read
Aloud, the Studio. Agreed with the writer on 26 November 2026 (README
decision 18):

- **Kinds.** A project has a kind, chosen when it is created and changeable
  later: **Novel** (novels and novellas, as today, and the default for every
  existing project), **Video series**, **Podcast**, **Editorial series**,
  **Journalism**.
- **Same shape for every kind.** A project still holds a main manuscript,
  novellas and chapters. A kind changes the words on screen (set by the
  kind, editable per project), which checks and goals apply, and what
  publishing prepares. A chapter is one video, one episode or one part.
- **Nothing a novelist has changes.** A Novel project looks and behaves
  exactly as it does before this phase (rule 11). Every test of today's
  behaviour keeps passing unchanged.
- **Phase F comes after the final sweep (9.1).** Sending anything to a site
  (YouTube and others) stays a later integration (decision 7); this phase
  prepares the material.

## The piece

- **Why:** the mistake that matters most in nonfiction is a fact nobody
  can back.
- **The writer gets**, for Journalism and Editorial series (offered for
  true-story scripts):
  - A check that marks **facts, figures, dates, quotes and named
    allegations with no source attached** (F.7's links).
  - A **claims report** in the Critique: every claim, its source, and the
    ones still open; exportable for an editor or a lawyer.
  - "Not a claim" and "Common knowledge" answers, remembered like "Not a
    problem" (4.1).
- **Tiers:** without a model, what can be found by pattern (numbers,
  dates, quotation marks, "according to", superlatives, named people with
  verbs of accusation). With a model, claims in plain sentences, marked
  as the model's.
- **The app never says a claim is true.** It says whether a source is
  attached. Say so on the report.
- Meets the four-in-five target before it is on by default (measure on
  real articles; put the figures in *Outcome*).

## Tests

- Made-up paragraphs with sourced and unsourced figures and quotes.
- The answers are remembered; the report matches the text.
- The model tier with a fake model.
- Off, and absent from the menus, in a Novel project.
