## The piece

- **Decided:** LanguageTool is used whenever it is available, and its larger
  language data is offered with the other downloads in configuration.
- **The writer gets:** in the downloads area of Settings, an entry for
  LanguageTool's extra data, with its size, a download button and a remove
  button. With it installed, the grammar check catches more confused words
  ("their" for "there"). Without it, everything works as today.
- **Read first:** `LanguageToolEngine` in `src/utils/proofing.py`, the downloads
  section of `src/ui/settings_dialog.py`.
- **Check first:** the exact data set LanguageTool needs, its size and its
  licence, from LanguageTool's own documentation.
- **Tests:** with the data absent, behaviour is unchanged; the benchmark shows
  more seeded confused words found with it present.
- **Done when:** the download can be added and removed from Settings and the
  Q.2 table shows its effect.

## What has changed since this brief was written

_Added 24 November 2026, after phase 3, D.3 and 2.5 were built. Read the Outcome of each piece named here._

- **Confused words are not "sure" rules.** They stay out of `SURE_RULES` in
  `src/ai/readiness_judge.py`, so the model may still clear them.
- **"Fix all N like this"** is now offered for any flag, including confused
  words, when the writer ticks its box (3.4, changed after review). With the
  larger data there will be more of these. Check the box's wording is clear
  enough that a writer does not change every "there" at once by accident;
  if not, leave confused words out of `fix_all.can_fix_everywhere`.
- **Measure with** `scripts/measure_misses.py` as well as the Q.2 table: it
  counts seeded confused words caught and flags on clean passages.
