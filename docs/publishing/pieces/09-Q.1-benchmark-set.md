## Phase context

Nothing in the app says how often a check is right. This phase builds that
measure. Every later claim about quality rests on it.

## The piece

- **Build:** a folder of test passages with the right answers recorded:
  - **Seeded errors.** Clean passages with known mistakes inserted: spelling,
    agreement, confused words, punctuation, repeated words. The right answer is
    the list of insertions.
  - **Clean published prose.** Passages of well-edited fiction in several
    genres, from the corpora the app can already fetch. The right answer is
    that almost nothing should be flagged.
  - **Marked style passages.** A small hand-marked set for the style checks and
    the overly descriptive check, including deliberate fragments, dialect and
    dialogue, where a flag would be wrong.
- **Read first:** `src/data/corpus_adapters.py`, `src/data/corpus_catalog.py`,
  `tests/test_proofing.py`.
- **Tiers:** no model needed to build it.
- **Done when:** the set exists, is small enough to run in a few minutes, and
  its answers are stored beside the passages.

## Outcome

**Status:** done. The hand-marked passages are a draft for the writer to
review (below).

**Built:** [`benchmark/`](../../../benchmark/README.md):

- **`clean/`**: 12 passages (4,210 words) of published fiction, one per
  book, in eight genres: literary, mystery, thriller, adventure, science
  fiction, western, horror and general. All are from 1898–1925, public
  domain in the US, and listed in `clean/manifest.json`.
- **`seeded/`**: the same passages with 58 known mistakes inserted: 12
  spelling, 12 confused words, 10 agreement, 12 punctuation and 12 repeated
  words. Each file stores the offsets and the change made.
- **`style/marked.json`**: 24 short passages written for the benchmark,
  with 73 hand marks. 30 are flags that would be **right**, and 43 are
  places where a flag would be **wrong**. Most passages come in pairs: the
  same pattern as a real weakness and as a deliberate choice (a passive that
  hides the actor in a fight against "was born"; an intensifier in
  narration against one in a character's speech; dull repeated openings
  against deliberate anaphora; a clumsy long sentence against a
  deliberately breathless one), plus dialect, fragments and clean prose.
  The passages are fully marked: every flag the checks raised on them was
  decided as right or wrong.
- **`scripts/build_benchmark.py`** rebuilds `clean/` and `seeded/` from the
  Gutenberg downloads the app keeps, giving the same output every time
  (fixed offsets and seed).

**Found while building:**

- **The corpus catalog's "Lovecraft" download is a Finnish book.** It was
  replaced by *The Willows* (Blackwood), and the builder now checks each
  book's own `Language:` header. The catalog entry itself is unchanged;
  fixing it is a one-line data fix outside this piece.
- One default passage was a page of broad Scots dialect (*The Thirty-Nine
  Steps*), so that book starts later.

**For the writer to review (about 15 minutes):** the marks in
`benchmark/style/marked.json`. Each has a `why`. They are one editor's
judgment, and a style check's score moves a lot on a single mark. The
calls most worth a second look:

- every "sticky sentence" flag on an ordinary sentence was marked wrong;
- "began to fall" (gradual) is marked as fine, while "began to run" is
  not;
- "finally", "nearly" and "immediately" are marked as adverbs that carry
  meaning;
- "Better late than never" as Gran's catchphrase is marked as fine.

**Tests:** `tests/test_benchmark.py` checks that every insertion is where
it says, every mark is in its passage, the clean set is English and spans
genres, and the scoring logic is right.
