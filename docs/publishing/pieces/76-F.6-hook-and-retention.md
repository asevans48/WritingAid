## Phase context

Phase F makes the app work for stories that are not novels, **keeping every
story feature**: plot, characters, world, critique, the Improve window, Read
Aloud, the Studio. Agreed with the writer on 26 November 2026 (README
decision 18):

- **Kinds.** A project has a kind, chosen when it is created and changeable
  later: **Novel** (novels and novellas, as today, and the default for every
  existing project), **Video series**, **Podcast**, **Editorial series**,
  **Journalism**.
- **Same shape for every kind.** A project still holds a main manuscript,
  novellas and chapters. A kind changes the words on screen (set by the
  kind, editable per project), which checks and goals apply, and what
  publishing prepares. A chapter is one video, one episode or one part.
- **Nothing a novelist has changes.** A Novel project looks and behaves
  exactly as it does before this phase (rule 11). Every test of today's
  behaviour keeps passing unchanged.
- **Phase F comes after the final sweep (9.1).** Sending anything to a site
  (YouTube and others) stays a later integration (decision 7); this phase
  prepares the material.

## The piece

- **Why:** a viewer or listener decides in the first half minute, and
  leaves at the first slow stretch.
- **The writer gets**, for Video series and Podcast projects, a report in
  the Critique (run like the other reports, saved in its history):
  - **The opening:** what the first 30 seconds (by the pace of F.3) say;
    is there a hook, and a promise of what the viewer will get?
  - **The promise kept:** the opening's promise against the ending,
    using the promises report that exists.
  - **Pacing:** stretches with no turn, question or reveal, on the
    tension curve and reverse outline that exist, shown in minutes.
  - **Between parts:** does each video or episode end on a reason to
    watch the next, and open with a way in for someone new?
  - **The ending:** a clear close and, if the writer wants one, a call to
    action.
- **Tiers (rules 1, 2 and 9):** without a model, the measurable parts
  (where the first question or turn falls, the length of flat stretches,
  whether the last lines point forward). With a model, a judgement of the
  hook and the promise, marked as the model's.
- Editorial series and Journalism may use the opening and ending parts
  (the lede and the kicker); say in *Outcome* if offered.

## Tests

- The no-model findings on made-up scripts with and without a hook, a
  flat middle, a cliffhanger.
- The model tier with a fake model; no real model runs.

## What has changed since this brief was written

_Added 26 November 2026, after 7.2 was built._

- **The tension curve exists** (7.2, `scene_tension`; drawn by 7.3's
  chart). It is one value per rules-based scene, 1 to 5, stored per
  chapter version; where a chapter has no scene breaks it is one value per
  chapter.

_Added 27 November 2026, after [7.4](59b-7.4-scene-markers.md) was built._

- **Scenes can be marked beside the text** (7.4, `src/data/scene_marks`).
  `reverse_outline.split_scenes(text, marks=…)` splits at the writer's
  scene markers as well as at break lines, so the tension curve has one
  value per marked scene. Any per-scene measure here should get its
  scenes the same way (`scene_marks.offsets(project, chapter_id, text)`),
  never from break lines alone; the text itself has no marker in it.

## When you finish

- The piece's own tests pass, then the full suite once.
- A Novel project from before the piece opens, looks and saves unchanged
  (a test proves it; rule 11).
- Screenshots from the harness at 1024 × 600, 1440 × 900, 2560 × 1440 and
  150% scaling for every screen the piece changes, for a Novel project and
  for one project of each kind the piece concerns.
- No count in `screens_baseline.json` rises for a Novel project (rule 13).
  A new kind's own figures are recorded and put to the writer.
- Fill in *Outcome* below and tick this piece in the [tracker](../README.md#tracker).
- Nothing is committed. Do not start the next piece.
- **Update [checklist.md](../checklist.md) in the same session:** tick this
  piece in section 5; add to section 1 the things the writer should try, as
  tick boxes; add to section 3 any decision needed; correct the counts and
  test totals at the top.
- If this piece changes something a later brief assumes, add a line to that
  brief's *What has changed since this brief was written* section.
- The full suite is run with: `HOME=$(mktemp -d) QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen venv/bin/python -m pytest -q -p no:cacheprovider`
