## The piece

- **This session:** the manuscript editor and its side panel, Suggestions,
  Rephrase, the critique tab, the style panel and Settings.
- **Do:** run the harness over the app's existing windows and dialogs, a group
  at a time. Fix what fails rule 12: add scrolling where content is squeezed
  or cut off, open windows within the screen, remove fixed heights on text.
- **Order:** the screens a writer uses most first: the manuscript editor and its
  side panel, Suggestions, Rephrase, the critique tab, the style panel,
  Settings. Then the rest.
- **Limit:** layout only. No change to what a screen does.
- **Done when:** the group passes the harness at all sizes. Repeat per group.

**What was wrong, and the fix**

- *Side panel 1,094 px, Write 971 px at 150 %:* not the Planner or the
  Companion, as D.6 guessed, but the bar under the text: "Words", the
  readability line ("Chapter: FK grade … · Reading ease …") and the grammar
  status are labels that would not shrink (794 px together). They are now
  `ElidedLabel`s: they show "…" when short of room and the whole line as a
  tip (the readability line keeps its own tip). The chapter list's
  "Right-click chapter for more options" is one too.
- *The Companion's note buttons* (Go to · Suggest fix · Mark beat done · Ask ·
  Dismiss) were one row of up to 366 px, which scrolled sideways at 150 %;
  they wrap now (`FlowLayout`).
- *Chapter Title:* a fixed 40 px height on text (its scroll bar showed at
  1024 and 150 %). It is now as tall as its text, one to three lines
  (`grow_with_text`), so a long title wraps and shows.
- *Toolbar:* the font size's "12" was cut (capped at 50 px with the app's
  8 px padding): slimmer side padding, cap 56 px. The heading box read "Norma"
  when the toolbar was short (capped 84–96 px): no cap, it is as wide as
  its longest item.
- *Critique in its own window:* the page could not scroll sideways, so at
  430 px the scorecard's header, legend and the rating row were cut. The
  legend and the rating row (`rating_bar.RatingBar`, used only by the
  Critique) wrap, "Check the book with the model…" wraps, the manuscript
  box elides a long name, and the page scrolls sideways if something still
  cannot narrow. Clicks on the scorecard are untouched (still handled after
  Qt finishes the event).
- *Stage chooser:* a long novella name widened it to 621 px; the heading
  and the chapter names wrap. The heading also showed `&#x27;` for an
  apostrophe (it was escaped as HTML but drawn as plain text): it is drawn

  3 px wider than its page and could not scroll sideways. It opens at
  980 × 680, fitted to the screen (`fit_to_screen`), minimum unchanged. Save
