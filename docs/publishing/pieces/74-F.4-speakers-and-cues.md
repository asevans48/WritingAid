## Phase context

Phase F makes the app work for stories that are not novels, **keeping every
story feature**: plot, characters, world, critique, the Improve window, Read
Aloud, the Studio. Agreed with the writer on 26 November 2026 (README
decision 18):

- **Kinds.** A project has a kind, chosen when it is created and changeable
  later: **Novel** (novels and novellas, as today, and the default for every
  existing project), **Video series**, **Podcast**, **Editorial series**,
  **Journalism**.
- **Same shape for every kind.** A project still holds a main manuscript,
  novellas and chapters. A kind changes the words on screen (set by the
  kind, editable per project), which checks and goals apply, and what
  publishing prepares. A chapter is one video, one episode or one part.
- **Nothing a novelist has changes.** A Novel project looks and behaves
  exactly as it does before this phase (rule 11). Every test of today's
  behaviour keeps passing unchanged.
- **Phase F comes after the final sweep (9.1).** Sending anything to a site
  (YouTube and others) stays a later integration (decision 7); this phase
  prepares the material.

## The piece

- **Why:** a script holds things that are not spoken (visuals, B-roll,
  music, ad breaks) and, for a podcast, more than one voice.
- **The writer gets**, for Video series and Podcast projects:
  - **Speakers:** a line can belong to a speaker (narrator, host,
    co-host, guest). Speakers are the project's People or Characters, so
    nothing is entered twice.
  - **Cues:** a marked line or span that is not spoken: visual, B-roll,
    music, sound, ad break, note. Shown in a quiet colour.
  - Cues are **not** read by Read Aloud or Listen and mark, **not**
    counted in words or minutes, and **not** checked as prose.
  - A cue list for a chapter (what is needed on screen and when), which
    the Studio and the video package (F.11) can use.
- **How it is stored:** in the chapter's text in a plain, readable
  marking that survives export and import and that an older version of
  the app shows as ordinary text (F.0 decides the marking). Text still
  reaches a chapter only through the one existing way.
- **Adding one:** from the right-click and a key, with no new toolbar
  button (rule 13).
- **Novel projects** are untouched: the marking is not treated specially
  there.

## Tests

- Cues and speaker labels are skipped by Read Aloud, the counts and the
  prose checks, and kept by save, export and import.
- A chapter with cues opens in a Novel project as plain text, unchanged.
- The cue list matches the chapter.
