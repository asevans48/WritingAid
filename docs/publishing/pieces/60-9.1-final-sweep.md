## The piece

- **The writer gets:** one copyedit pass over the whole book: every remaining
  consistency issue and style sheet break in a single list, fixed one at a
  time or by kind.
- **Done when:** the copyedit columns of the scorecard are clear after the sweep.

## What has changed since this brief was written

_Added 24 November 2026, after phase 3, D.3 and 2.5 were built. Read the Outcome of each piece named here._

- **Most of the mechanism exists.** "Fixed one at a time" is the next-fix
  queue at the copyedit stage (`fix_queue.entries(report, stage="copyedit")`,
  3.2). "By kind" is "Fix all N like this" (3.4).
- **What is missing:** both work on one chapter. This piece adds the walk
  across chapters: the queue moving to the next chapter with copyedit
  findings, and a fix-all that covers the book. 3.4 left that out on
  purpose, because other chapters are not open in the editor and one undo
  could not reverse them. Solve that here (for example chapter by chapter,
  each with its own undo and a list of what changed), within rule 15.
- **Stages:** offer the sweep when every chapter is at copyedit or later
  (`chapter_stages`); do not move stages for the writer.

- **Added 25 November 2026 by D.5.** Analyze ▾ runs per-chapter reports in the Critique, recorded in critique history under their own buckets (`analysis:<entry>:<manuscript>:<chapter>`, new kinds under `analyses`). A sweep that reads history for "what is left" should read `reports` and `analyses` (`critique_history._all_reports`) and leave those buckets apart from full-critique runs.
- **Added 25 November 2026 by 4.3.** The queue's Accept, Skip and Not a
  problem, and "Fix all N like this" (N accepts), are counted per check in
  the readiness file (`check_outcomes.record`); a sweep that accepts fixes
  should count them the same way (one accept per place changed, through
  `check_outcomes_ui.accepted` / `fixed_everywhere`), not as one. Checks
  the writer mostly turns down come later in `fix_queue.entries` when given
  `toned=check_outcomes.toned_down(project)`; the sweep should pass it too.

## Build notes

_Added 24 November 2026 so a fresh session can finish this piece without searching. Names were checked against the code on that date; if one has moved, find it with a search and say so in Outcome._

- **Decided 24 November 2026: chapter by chapter.** The sweep opens each
  chapter in turn and makes changes through the editor, so each chapter has
  its own undo. There is no one-click change to chapters that are not open.
- **Read first:** `Queue` in `src/ui/next_fixes.py` (`find`, `accept`,
  `chapter_loaded`); `entries` in `src/ai/fix_queue.py`; `open_chapter` in
  `src/ui/revision_controller.py`; `src/ui/fix_all.py`; `book_report` in
  `src/ai/readiness.py`; `src/ui/consistency_dialog.py`.
- **Build:**
  - **Start:** "Final sweep" as a link on the scorecard's Whole manuscript
    row, shown when every chapter is at copyedit or later.
  - **The walk:** the queue, with stage "copyedit", gains "Next chapter with
    copyedit findings →" when the current chapter's queue is empty. It
    opens that chapter (`open_chapter`) and carries on. A line shows
    "chapter 4 of 12, 31 left in the book".
  - **By kind across the book:** "Fix all N like this" stays per chapter.
    After it runs, the card offers "The same in the next chapter (5
    more)", which opens the next chapter and shows the Fix-all list there.
    The writer confirms each chapter.
  - **A record:** the sweep keeps a list of what it changed, chapter by
    chapter, shown at the end and saved beside the readiness file.
- **Nothing here writes a chapter file directly** (rule 15).
- **Tests:** with three chapters holding copyedit findings, the walk visits
  each in order; undo in a chapter reverses that chapter's changes; a
  chapter the writer skips is left exactly as it was; the record lists
  every change.
