## Phase context

Phase F makes the app work for stories that are not novels, **keeping every
story feature**: plot, characters, world, critique, the Improve window, Read
Aloud, the Studio. Agreed with the writer on 26 November 2026 (README
decision 18):

- **Kinds.** A project has a kind, chosen when it is created and changeable
  later: **Novel** (novels and novellas, as today, and the default for every
  existing project), **Video series**, **Podcast**, **Editorial series**,
  **Journalism**.
- **Same shape for every kind.** A project still holds a main manuscript,
  novellas and chapters. A kind changes the words on screen (set by the
  kind, editable per project), which checks and goals apply, and what
  publishing prepares. A chapter is one video, one episode or one part.
- **Nothing a novelist has changes.** A Novel project looks and behaves
  exactly as it does before this phase (rule 11). Every test of today's
  behaviour keeps passing unchanged.
- **Phase F comes after the final sweep (9.1).** Sending anything to a site
  (YouTube and others) stays a later integration (decision 7); this phase
  prepares the material.

## The piece

- **Why:** a script is judged in minutes, not words.
- **The writer gets**, for Video series and Podcast projects:
  - Minutes beside the word count for each chapter, each manuscript and
    the project, at the writer's speaking pace (a setting; start at 150
    words a minute; "measure my pace" from a Read Aloud or a recording is
    a later step).
  - A target length per video or episode and for the series (for example
    "18 to 25 minutes"), where a Novel has a word-count goal. The
    readiness and goal screens show progress against it.
  - Cues and speaker labels (F.4) are not counted.
- **The writing habit (H.1)** keeps counting words written and revised
  for every kind; it is about the habit, not the length.
- **Novel, Editorial series and Journalism** keep words (Editorial and
  Journalism may show reading time; say so in *Outcome* if added).
- No new control on the Write screen: the minutes sit where the word
  count is.

## Tests

- Minutes follow the words and the pace; cues are left out.
- A target in minutes saves, reopens and shows progress.
- A Novel project shows no minutes and its goals are unchanged.
