## The piece

- **Status:** optional. The list of what to build now comes from ProWritingAid's
  own pages (see [background.md](../background.md)). This piece adds a check
  of quality, not of features.
- **Your part:** run three of your own chapters through ProWritingAid and export
  or copy what it reports.
- **The session's part:** run the same chapters through this app, then list what
  each caught that the other missed, and where each was wrong.
- **Done when:** the list is in `docs/` and P.5 is updated from it.
