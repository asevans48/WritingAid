## The piece

- **The writer gets:** each mark appears as a note on its sentence in the
  Companion, where **Suggest fix** already works.
- **Done when:** marks made while listening can be fixed from the notes section.

## What has changed since this brief was written

_Added 24 November 2026, after phase 3, D.3 and 2.5 were built. Read the Outcome of each piece named here._

- **Put marks in the next-fix queue,** not only the notes section. The queue
  takes entries from outside the readiness report through `extra`
  (`fix_queue.entries`), the way the style flags arrive
  (`fix_queue.style_entries`). A mark becomes an entry with its sentence as
  the place and no suggested fix, so "More options" offers the rewrite.
- **Stage:** marks made while listening are line-stage entries unless the
  writer says otherwise.

## Outcome

**Built 25 November 2026. Not yet tried by the writer.** No model is
called anywhere; the model tier is tested with a fake.

**What the writer does:** marks sentences with 🗣 Read Aloud ▾ → 👂 Listen
and mark (6.1). When listening stops, each mark shows in the Companion in
two places, both read from the one store (`listen_marks`, R.7 companion
file):

- **Notes as you write:** a 👂 note, "Marked while listening: “…”", on its
  sentence, in paragraph order with the other notes. **💡 Suggest fix**
  opens Suggestions on the sentence, asked to fix how it sounds (rhythm,
  clarity, wording, flow; meaning kept). **Go to**, **💬 Ask** work as for any
  note. **Dismiss** clears the mark.
- **Next fixes (the queue):** a line-stage entry, "Marked while listening",
  with the sentence as its place and no quick fix. So the card offers
  **✎ edit** and **💡 More options…** (Suggestions); with a model, the card
  writes rewrites in the writer's voice as it does for any finding
  (`suggest_fix_options`). It ranks first among line-stage entries of the
  same standing (tier "major"), because the writer marked it.

**Clearing a mark:** fixing it (a change accepted from Suggest fix, from
More options…, or with ✎ edit then Accept on the card) or dismissing it
(Dismiss in the notes, or **Not a problem** on the card) removes it from
the store, the notes and the queue together. Not a problem on a mark clears
that one mark only. Nothing is remembered against the pattern, and the
"not a problem" count (4.3) is not touched. **Skip** keeps it.
Turning down Suggestions keeps it. A mark whose sentence has changed stays
a note with "(the text has changed)" until the writer dismisses it. It is
left out of the queue, which needs the sentence to point at.

**Stage:** marks are line-stage entries. A chapter at another stage does
not show them in the queue, but they always show in the notes. The brief's
"unless the writer says otherwise" is not built (no new control): see
decisions.

**Where it is (rule 13):** nothing added on screen. Marks use the notes
section's and the card's existing buttons. Counts unchanged: Companion
buttons 15 (15), sections 7 (7); Write buttons 19 (19), menu items 29 (29);
right-click 9 / 13 (9 / 13).

**Files:**

- new `src/ui/heard_notes.py`: `HeardNote` (a `companion.Note` of kind
  "heard" with `mark_id` and `focus`), `notes_for`, `entries_for` (no Qt

  `queue_extra`, `entry_done`, `queue_dismiss`, `refresh`.
