## The piece

- **The writer gets:** each mark appears as a note on its sentence in the
  Companion, where **Suggest fix** already works.
- **Done when:** marks made while listening can be fixed from the notes section.

## What has changed since this brief was written

_Added 24 November 2026, after phase 3, D.3 and 2.5 were built. Read the Outcome of each piece named here._

- **Put marks in the next-fix queue,** not only the notes section. The queue
  takes entries from outside the readiness report through `extra`
  (`fix_queue.entries`), the way the style flags arrive
  (`fix_queue.style_entries`). A mark becomes an entry with its sentence as
  the place and no suggested fix, so "More options" offers the rewrite.
- **Stage:** marks made while listening are line-stage entries unless the
  writer says otherwise.

## Build notes

_Added 24 November 2026 so a fresh session can finish this piece without searching. Names were checked against the code on that date; if one has moved, find it with a search and say so in Outcome._

- **Read first:** `Note` in `src/ai/companion.py`; `set_notes` and the
  "Notes as you write" section (with "💡 Suggest fix") in
  `src/ui/companion_panel.py`; `entries` and `style_entries` in
  `src/ai/fix_queue.py`; `Queue._style_flags` in `src/ui/next_fixes.py`.
- **Build:** marks from 6.1 become `Note`s of a new kind, "heard", in the
  notes section, and queue entries through `extra`.
- **Clearing a mark:** fixing or dismissing it removes the mark from
  `companion_state`; skipping does not.
- **Tests:** a mark appears in the notes and in the queue at the line stage;
  Suggest fix works on it; a fixed mark does not return after a restart.
