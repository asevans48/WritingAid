## The piece

- **The writer gets:** anywhere a quirk is flagged (fragments, dialect, an
  unusual rhythm), a **This is intentional** choice that adds it to the style
  sheet.
- **Builds on:** 3.3. This piece adds the choice to the editor's right-click
  menu and the scorecard's item list.
- **Done when:** a quirk marked intentional is never flagged again.

## What has changed since this brief was written

_Added 24 November 2026, after phase 3, D.3 and 2.5 were built. Read the Outcome of each piece named here._

- **Most of this exists.** Three ways to say "leave it" are built:
  - **That's intended** on a finding in the Critique (the editor's queries):
    stored as dismissed, listed in the editor's brief.
  - **Not a problem** on the next-fix card (3.3): the pattern is dismissed in
    this project, in every chapter (`fix_queue.dismiss`, stored with
    `"as": "not a problem"`), and kept out of the editor's brief.
  - **Ignore** on the hover card, and "Ignore this issue" for style flags.
- **What is left for this piece:** "This is intentional" in the editor's
  right-click menu (which needs D.1, since the Write tab does not show the
  flag section today), and writing the quirk to the **style sheet** so it is
  a rule of the book, not only a dismissed flag.
- **Do not add a fourth store.** Use `readiness_state`'s `"dismissed"` and the
  style sheet. The Critique already lists both with "take that back".
