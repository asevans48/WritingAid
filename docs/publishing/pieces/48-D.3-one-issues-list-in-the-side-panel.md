## Phase context

Each of these lowers the counts recorded in 0.2. All follow rule 11:

- Each piece starts with a **capability checklist** of everything the old
  screen can do, and is not done until each item has a home in the new one.
- The replacement is built **alongside** the old screen. The old one is retired
  in a separate small step, after you have used the new one on both projects
  and agreed.
- Anything moved leaves a pointer, and shortcuts keep working.

## The piece

- **The writer gets:** the right-hand panel goes from four tabs to three. The
  Style tab's list of issues becomes the line stage of the Companion's queue.
  Its on and off switches for highlighting move to a small "Highlights"
  control on the editor's toolbar.
- **Read first:** `src/ui/style_panel.py`, the right panel set-up in
  `src/ui/manuscript_editor.py`.
- **Also consider in 0.2:** whether the Feedback tab and the Companion's notes
  overlap enough to merge.
- **Done when:** every style issue can be reached and fixed from the queue and
  the highlight switches work from the toolbar. The Style tab stays until you
  agree to retire it; it then goes with a one-time note saying where it went.

- **Data safety:** issues the writer has marked as ignored, and the style sheet
  rules behind them, are read from where they are today and still honoured.
  The highlight switches keep their saved state.
