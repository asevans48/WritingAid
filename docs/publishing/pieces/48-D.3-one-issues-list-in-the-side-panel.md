## The piece

- **The writer gets:** the right-hand panel goes from four tabs to three
  (Planner, Companion, Feedback). The Style tab's list of issues becomes the
  line stage of the Companion's queue. Its switch for each of the eleven
  checks moves into the existing **✓ Checks ▾** menu on the editor toolbar,
  as a "Style checks" submenu. That menu already turns spelling, grammar,
  overuse and style on and off. No new toolbar control is added (agreed in
  0.2).
- **Read first:** `src/ui/style_panel.py`, the right panel set-up in
  `src/ui/manuscript_editor.py`, and the Checks menu set-up in
  `ChapterEditor` (same file).
- **Settled in 0.2:** the Feedback tab stays its own tab. It holds the writer's
  notes from readers and editors, which are input, not a list of the app's
  findings, so it does not overlap the Companion's notes.
- **Capability checklist** (everything the Style tab does today, and where it
  goes):
  - counts per check → queue section header, and the Checks submenu;
  - toggle each check's highlighting → Checks → Style checks;
  - step through issues → the queue;
  - apply the suggested fix → the queue's Accept;
  - ✨ Rewrite with AI → the queue, opening the same rewrite;
  - Ignore → the queue's "Not a problem" (3.3), or Ignore until 3.3 is built;
  - open Consistency → still in the Checks menu, the Analyze menu and the
    Review menu.
- **Shortcuts stay:** "Style issues panel…" in the Checks menu opens the
  Companion on the queue once the Style tab is retired.
- **Done when:** every style issue can be reached and fixed from the queue and
  the highlight switches work from the toolbar. The Style tab stays until you
  agree to retire it; it then goes with a one-time note saying where it went.

- **Data safety:** issues the writer has marked as ignored, and the style sheet
  rules behind them, are read from where they are today and still honoured.
  The highlight switches keep their saved state.
