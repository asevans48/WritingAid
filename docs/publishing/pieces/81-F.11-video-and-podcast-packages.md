## Phase context

Phase F makes the app work for stories that are not novels, **keeping every
story feature**: plot, characters, world, critique, the Improve window, Read
Aloud, the Studio. Agreed with the writer on 26 November 2026 (README
decision 18):

- **Kinds.** A project has a kind, chosen when it is created and changeable
  later: **Novel** (novels and novellas, as today, and the default for every
  existing project), **Video series**, **Podcast**, **Editorial series**,
  **Journalism**.
- **Same shape for every kind.** A project still holds a main manuscript,
  novellas and chapters. A kind changes the words on screen (set by the
  kind, editable per project), which checks and goals apply, and what
  publishing prepares. A chapter is one video, one episode or one part.
- **Nothing a novelist has changes.** A Novel project looks and behaves
  exactly as it does before this phase (rule 11). Every test of today's
  behaviour keeps passing unchanged.
- **Phase F comes after the final sweep (9.1).** Sending anything to a site
  (YouTube and others) stays a later integration (decision 7); this phase
  prepares the material.

## The piece

- **Why:** a video or an episode is published with words around it, as a
  novel is submitted with a synopsis and a query.
- **The writer gets**, in Publish, where the submission package is (9.3)
  and built on it:
  - **Video package**, per video and for a series: title options, a
    description, tags, **chapter timestamps** from the script's headings
    and the pace (F.3), thumbnail text, a pinned-comment line, and the
    cue list (F.4).
  - **Podcast package**, per episode and for a season: episode title,
    summary, show notes with timestamps, the speakers, and a
    **transcript** export (the script with speaker labels, cues left
    out).
- **Tiers, as 9.3:** filled in from the script and plan without a model
  (bracketed where something is missing); "Draft in my voice" with one.
  Earlier versions kept; stored beside the project; the script is never
  changed.
- A Novel project's Publish screen and its submission package are
  unchanged. A kind sees its own package in the same place, so the
  counts do not rise.
- When the real video exists (F.10), timestamps can be corrected by
  hand; reading them from the video is a later step.
