## The piece

- **The writer gets:** each check shows how often its fixes are accepted. A
  check the writer mostly rejects is shown less prominently, with the reason
  given and a way to bring it back.
- **Build:** count accept, skip and not-a-problem per check, per project, in
  `readiness_state`. This works whether or not training-data collection is on.
- **Done when:** the acceptance rate appears in each column's tooltip and a
  rejected check is toned down.

## What has changed since this brief was written

_Added 24 November 2026, after phase 3, D.3 and 2.5 were built. Read the Outcome of each piece named here._

- **Where to count:** the three actions are in one place now, `Queue.accept`,
  `Queue.skip` and `Queue.dismiss` in `src/ui/next_fixes.py` (3.2, 3.3). Also
  count "Fix all N like this" as N accepts (`fix_all.everywhere`), and
  "That's intended" in the Critique as not-a-problem.
- **Skip is for the session only** (it moves the finding to the back). Count
  it, but do not treat a skip as a rejection.
- **Toning a check down** must also lower it in the queue's order
  (`fix_queue.entries` sorts by the check's `weight`).
- **4.1 is built.** Every "leave it" now goes through `src/ai/intentional.py`
  (`store`, `mark`, `unmark`), and each dismissal says which it was in
  `"as"`: `"not a problem"` (the card's Not a problem) or `"intended"` (the
  Critique's That's intended, the editor's right-click "📌 This is
  intentional", the card's "It's intentional" link). An entry with no `"as"`
  was written by the Critique before 4.1: treat it as intended. Count
  "intended" as not-a-problem too, or as its own count, but read it from
  that one place; the right-click entry never passes through `Queue`.
- **Added 24 November 2026 by P.3.** Adverbs, passive voice, sentence
  variety, pacing and chapter length can be measured against published
  books in the genre (`src/ai/genre_baseline.py`). A check toned down for a
  writer should still compare with the genre where it has figures.
- **Added 25 November 2026 by P.2b.** Four more style kinds reach the
  queue when ticked (`homonym`, `sensory`, `sentence_structure`, `vague`,
  in `fix_queue.STYLE_ONLY_KINDS`); count them like the other style kinds.
  A homonym's card fix is one place only: it never goes through
  `fix_all.everywhere`.

  `src/ai/fix_queue.py` (the order).
- **Store** under a new top-level key in the readiness file, for example
  `"outcomes": {check: {"accepted": n, "skipped": n, "not_a_problem": n}}`.
  A missing key means zeros (rule 11).
- **"Toned down"** means: lower in the queue, and its column drawn fainter
  with the reason in the tooltip. Never hidden, never switched off. "Bring
  it back" clears the toning, not the counts.
- **A minimum before judging:** no check is toned down on fewer than about
  twenty answers.
- **Tests:** counts survive a restart; a check with mostly "not a problem"
  is toned down and brought back; an old readiness file with no counts
  loads.

## Outcome

**Built 25 November 2026.** Waiting for the writer's review and the rule 15
safety check (before and after) on both projects: it adds a key to each
project's readiness file.

**What is counted, and where.** Per check, per project, in the project's
readiness file (`<project>.readiness.json`) under a new top-level key
`"outcomes"`: `{check: {"accepted", "skipped", "not_a_problem", "intended",
"restored": {"accepted", "rejected"}}}`. Only added: a file without it means
zeros, damaged values read as zeros, every other key is kept, an older app
ignores it. Counts are never cleared. It is counted whether or not
training-data collection is on.

- **accepted:** the next-fix card's Accept (`Queue.accept`), and "Fix all N
  like this" as N (`fix_all.everywhere`, and the "See all N…" list,
  `fix_all.offer`).
- **skipped:** Skip on the card. Counted, never read as a rejection.
- **not_a_problem / intended:** read from the one place every "leave it"
  goes through, `intentional.store`, which now counts a pattern the first
  time it is dismissed, in the same save as the dismissal. So the card's
  Not a problem, the Critique's That's intended, the right-click
  "📌 This is intentional" and the card's "It's intentional" link are all
  counted, once each. The card's Not a problem on a style flag (a session
  ignore, never stored) is counted by the card. Undo right after Not a
  problem takes the count back (a misclick); "take that back" later in the
  Critique keeps it (it is history). "It's intentional" after Not a
  problem is not counted twice.
- **Which check:** the queue's check name (`entry.check`: a readiness check
  such as `filler`, or `style:<kind>` for the kinds no check reports). The
  right-click entry and Fix all find it from the flag
  (`check_outcomes.check_for_key` / `check_for_error`: spelling and grammar
  → `spelling_grammar`, overused words → `repetition`, a style kind → its
  check, e.g. intensifier → `filler`); the Critique's That's intended by
  the check's label.

**Toned down.** A check is toned down when, since it was last brought
back, the writer has answered at least 20 of its fixes
(`MIN_ANSWERS`) and accepted fewer than two in five (`TONE_BELOW`). Then:

- **Queue:** its findings come after the others of their stage
  (`fix_queue.entries(..., toned=)`; the order key gains "toned" after the
  stage). Never hidden.
- **Critique:** each check column's tooltip gives "Fixes accepted here: N
  of M (x%); k not a problem or intended, s skipped." (or "No fixes
  answered yet in this project."). A toned-down column's header and cells
  are drawn fainter, with the reason in the tooltip; the check's details
  below the grid say why, with **Bring it back**.
- **Card:** a toned-down check's card adds "toned down for this book: bring
  it back" to its status line.
- **✓ Checks ▾ › Style checks:** each switch's tooltip gives its check's
  rate; a toned-down one has "· toned down" after its name.
- **Bring it back** stores the counts as they are under `"restored"`; the
  check is judged again on answers after that. The counts stay.
- Genre comparison (P.3) is untouched: toning changes order and colour,
  not a result or its message.

**Switching off is only offered.** When the writer's Not a problem on the
card tones a check down, the status line says "You've turned down 20 of 20
Filler and weak intensifiers fixes in this book, so it's toned down …
**Switch it off** · **Keep it as it was**". Switch it off appears only for a
check with ✓ Checks ▾ switches, never for spelling and grammar or overused
words (decision 3: `check_outcomes.STAYS_ON`). Clicking it unticks the same
tick boxes as the menu (so it is remembered per project by
`check_switches`); nothing changes the switches without that click.
