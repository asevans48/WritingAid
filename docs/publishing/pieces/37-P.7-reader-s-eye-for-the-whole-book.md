## The piece

- **Parity with:** ProWritingAid's virtual beta reader.
- **The writer gets:** across the manuscript, where a reader is hooked, where
  they are likely to put the book down, and the emotional effect of each
  chapter, shown as one more row of marks on the scorecard and as a section of
  the editorial letter.
- **Build:** extend the Companion's existing reader's eye from one chapter to a
  manuscript, reusing each chapter's cached result.
- **Tiers:** needs a model. Without one, the scorecard shows "needs a model" for
  this row and it is left out of the readiness figure.
- **Done when:** hook and drop-off points appear per chapter for a main
  manuscript and for a novella.
