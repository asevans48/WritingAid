## Phase context

Phase F makes the app work for stories that are not novels, **keeping every
story feature**: plot, characters, world, critique, the Improve window, Read
Aloud, the Studio. Agreed with the writer on 26 November 2026 (README
decision 18):

- **Kinds.** A project has a kind, chosen when it is created and changeable
  later: **Novel** (novels and novellas, as today, and the default for every
  existing project), **Video series**, **Podcast**, **Editorial series**,
  **Journalism**.
- **Same shape for every kind.** A project still holds a main manuscript,
  novellas and chapters. A kind changes the words on screen (set by the
  kind, editable per project), which checks and goals apply, and what
  publishing prepares. A chapter is one video, one episode or one part.
- **Nothing a novelist has changes.** A Novel project looks and behaves
  exactly as it does before this phase (rule 11). Every test of today's
  behaviour keeps passing unchanged.
- **Phase F comes after the final sweep (9.1).** Sending anything to a site
  (YouTube and others) stays a later integration (decision 7); this phase
  prepares the material.

## The piece

The fit-and-scroll sweeps (U.3) and the final sweep (9.1) were done for
novels. This repeats them for the new kinds.

- **The harness draws every screen for one project of each kind** (a
  sample Video series, Podcast, Editorial series and Journalism project,
  with long renamed words, cues, sources and a chapter with a video), at
  1024 × 600, 1440 × 900, 2560 × 1440 and 150%.
- **Fit and scroll (rule 12):** every screen fits and scrolls with the
  longer words ("Editorial series", "Episode 12", a renamed word of 20
  letters). Nothing is cut off or overlaps.
- **Words:** no "Chapter", "Novella" or "Manuscript" is left on screen
  in a project of another kind, except where the word is meant (the kind
  list itself).
- **Counts (rule 13):** each kind's screens are counted; none is above
  the Novel's figure for the same screen unless the writer agreed it.
  The agreed figures go in `screens_baseline.json` under the kind.
- **The safety check (0.3)** opens, saves and compares a project of each
  kind.
- **The final-sweep list of 9.1**, run again for each kind: every path
  through the app (create, write, check, critique, improve, listen,
  Studio, publish, export) works end to end.
- **A Novel project:** the U.3 and 9.1 results are unchanged.

## What has changed since this brief was written

_Added 26 November 2026 by [U.3b](51-U.3b-fit-and-scroll-sweep-the-remaining-screens.md)._

- **Narrow rows: shorten, then wrap.** Rows of buttons are
  `ui_kit.FlowLayout`s; a button's narrow words are set with
  `ui_kit.short_label` and read back with `ui_kit.full_text`, never
  `text()`. Where a kind's word ("Chapter", "Episode") is in a button that
  has short words, give the short words from the same place as the long
  ones. U.3b's Outcome lists the screens that could not take a longer word.
- **The harness draws more:** each D.4 screen at its tiled share
  (`<screen>_tiled`) and twelve windows that were taller than 600 px
  (`DIALOG_SCREENS`). Sweep the new kinds on those too.

_Added 26 November 2026 from [F.0's design](70-F.0-project-kinds-design.md) (section "Changes the other phase F briefs need"; read the design first)._

- the sample projects include a true-story Video series. The
  safety check covers `kind.json` and `sources.json`.
