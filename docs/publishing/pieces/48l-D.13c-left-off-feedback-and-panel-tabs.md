## The piece

The writer (27 Nov): "Let's move the details where you left off to the
ribbon. Can we incorporate our feedback updates from other sections in the
Feedback tab? Please use the full text for each tab. They are cut off when
there is enough space to display the name; they should only cut off when
space is too limited." Their answers:

- **Which tabs:** the side panel's tabs (Planner · Feedback · Companion),
  "the details sidebar where we see where you left off, feedback, find the
  next fixes and other companion behaviour". Also: "currently the sidebar
  only opens for its own row" (see 1).
- **Where you left off:** a line on the ribbon's right.
- **Feedback from elsewhere:** Companion notes, Critique findings, the
  Improve window.

1. **"Where you left off" on the ribbon.** The one-line banner above the
   text (`CompanionBanner`) moves to the right end of the ribbon's row,
   shown on every tab: its text shortened with "…" only when there is no
   room (hover shows all of it), then **Details**. The banner's line above
   the text goes, so the text gains that height (measure it). Its ✕ (hide
   until the chapter is next opened) stays as it is. **Details** opens the
   side panel on the Companion (and Hide details closes it), as now.
   - "The sidebar only opens for its own row": find out what the writer
     sees (likely the side panel taking only the editor's row, or Details
     opening just the Companion's own section). Make Details open the
     whole side panel, at full height beside the ribbon and the text, on
     the Companion tab, scrolled to "Where you left off". Say in the
     Outcome what was wrong and what changed.
2. **One Feedback tab.** The Feedback tab keeps what it has (feedback the
   writer adds with + Feedback, and Analyze), and also lists, for the open
   chapter, read-only and each with where it came from:
   - **Companion notes** ("Notes as you write" and Reader's eye results);
   - **Critique findings** for this chapter (Revise ▸ Critique: its
     readiness checks and reports);
   - **Improve window** critiques and suggestions for passages in this
     chapter (what the window already stores or logs; nothing new asked of
     a model).
   Each item has Go to (the passage, where there is one) and opens its
   source (the Companion, the Critique, the Improve window). Group them by
   source under small headings that fold, newest first, with counts; the
   writer's own feedback first. Nothing is moved out of the Companion, the
   Critique or the Improve window (rule 11): the Feedback tab is one more
   place to read them. Dismissing in the source hides it here too. No new
   stored data unless needed; nothing is written to the project from
   reading.
3. **Whole tab names.** The side panel's tab names (and the ribbon's) show
   in full whenever there is room, and shorten only when the panel is too
   narrow for them. Check the cause on macOS's own style too (the harness
   draws Qt's own style offscreen, which may not show it: for example the
   side panel's `font-size: 11px` style sheet on `QTabBar::tab`, an elide
   mode, or the "Hide ✕" corner taking the width). Fix the cause, not the
   symptom; a test checks that each tab's width fits its whole name at the
   panel's usual width. Apply the same to any other tab bar with the same
   style sheet.

- **Rules:** 11 (nothing cut), 12 (fits at 1024 × 600 and 150 %), 13 (no
  count rises without the writer's say: the Feedback tab's headings are
  sections; report the figures), 14, 15.
- **Done when:** the line is on the ribbon and the banner row is gone;
  Details opens the whole panel on the Companion; the Feedback tab shows
  the three sources for the open chapter; tab names are whole unless the
  panel is too narrow; the harness passes; the full suite passes.

**3. Whole tab names** (`src/ui/tab_names.py`). **The cause:** the side
panel's style sheet set the names' size on the tab only (`QTabBar::tab {
font-size: 11px }`). Qt measures each tab, and decides whether a name must
be shortened, with the tab **bar's** own font (13 px, from the app's
`QWidget` rule), but draws it with the tab's 11 px. On macOS a tab bar
shortens names that do not fit (`ElideRight` is the Mac style's default),
so "Planner" measured at 13 px in a tab sized for 11 px became "Plan…",
"Feedb…", "Companio…" with room to spare. Qt's own style, which the harness
draws with offscreen, never shortens (and scrolls instead), so the
pictures never showed it. Checked on the Mac's own style (an off-screen
render, nothing shown): before, "Plan… Feedb… Companio…" at 301 px and at
468 px; after, "Planner Feedback Companion (12)" at both, shortened only at
240 px. **The fix:** the bar gets the same font as its tabs (`QTabBar,
QTabBar::tab { font-size: 11px; font-weight: 500 }`, the weight the app
gives tab names), so a name is measured as it is drawn; and every platform
now behaves alike: names shorten with "…" only when the bar is really too
narrow, never hidden behind scroll arrows. The "Hide ✕" corner was not the
cause (the names fit beside it at the panel's narrowest, 300 px). Applied
to the other tab bars with the same kind of sheet: the main window's AI
Assistant / Chapter Outline tabs (a call line in `main_window.py`) and the
AI debug panel's tabs. The ribbon's names were already whole (they never
shorten); its bar now has its tabs' weight too. The app-wide rule
(`QTabBar::tab { font-size: 14px }` against `QWidget`'s 13 px) was checked
on the Mac's style: its 24 px padding leaves every main tab whole, so
`styles.py` is unchanged.
