## Phase context

Phase F makes the app work for stories that are not novels, **keeping every
story feature**: plot, characters, world, critique, the Improve window, Read
Aloud, the Studio. Agreed with the writer on 26 November 2026 (README
decision 18):

- **Kinds.** A project has a kind, chosen when it is created and changeable
  later: **Novel** (novels and novellas, as today, and the default for every
  existing project), **Video series**, **Podcast**, **Editorial series**,
  **Journalism**.
- **Same shape for every kind.** A project still holds a main manuscript,
  novellas and chapters. A kind changes the words on screen (set by the
  kind, editable per project), which checks and goals apply, and what
  publishing prepares. A chapter is one video, one episode or one part.
- **Nothing a novelist has changes.** A Novel project looks and behaves
  exactly as it does before this phase (rule 11). Every test of today's
  behaviour keeps passing unchanged.
- **Phase F comes after the final sweep (9.1).** Sending anything to a site
  (YouTube and others) stays a later integration (decision 7); this phase
  prepares the material.

## The piece

The writer, 26 November 2026: "when uploading a video, the video is
uploaded to the Studio. it is for a selected chapter and becomes editable
in the studio. people should be able to refine the card that is created
in the studio."

- **The writer gets:**
  - **Add a video…** on a chapter (the tree's right-click, D.10) and on
    the project or a manuscript. Choose a video file: it goes to the
    Studio as a **card for that chapter** (for a project or manuscript,
    a card of its own), and the Studio opens on it.
  - The card is refined with the Studio's editors that exist (the card
    editor, the video editor, audio and voice-over): title and subtitle,
    trim, audio, transitions.
  - The chapter shows that it has a video (a small mark in the tree) and
    opens it in the Studio from there. A chapter can have more than one.
  - For every kind, Novel included (a novelist's trailer or reading).
- **Read the Studio first.** It already has cards with a video
  background or element, clip import and chapter decks
  (`card_editor_dialog.py`, `chapter_deck_editor.py`,
  `studio_widget.py`). Reuse them: this piece is the way in from a
  chapter and the link between a chapter and its card, not a second
  editor.
- **The file (rule 15):** the Studio's existing rule for media decides
  whether the video is copied into the project or linked. Say which in
  *Outcome*. Nothing is overwritten; a missing file is reported on the
  card, and the chapter and card are kept. Large files are never put in
  the project file itself.
- **Counts (rule 13):** one entry on the chapter's right-click, grouped
  with the Studio's existing entries if there are any; no new button.
- **Later, not here:** turning a video's speech into a chapter's text,
  and sending a finished video to a site (decision 7).

## Tests

- Add a video to a chapter: a card for that chapter exists in the
  Studio, the chapter is marked, save and reopen keep both.
- A second video; removing the card leaves the file and the chapter.
- A missing file is reported and nothing is lost.
- Moving the chapter to another manuscript (D.10) keeps its video.
- Use small made-up media files in `tmp_path`; no real encoding run
  beyond what the Studio's tests do today.
