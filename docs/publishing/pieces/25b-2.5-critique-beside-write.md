## The piece

- **Asked for:** "In the critiquer, we want a link to the failure or issue so
  we can see what the problem is. Can we also allow users to pop out the
  critique module so they can place write and critique side by side."
- **The writer gets:**
  - every finding in the Critique's issue list links to its place in the
    chapter, and a finding repeated in several places links to each;
  - a failing check says what it looks for;
  - the Critique in its own window, beside Write.
- **Done when:** a finding's link opens the chapter with the passage
  selected, with the Critique in its tab or in its own window; closing the
  window puts the Critique back in its tab with nothing lost.

## Outcome

**Built 24 November 2026. Not yet tried by the writer on a real project.**

**Links** (`src/ui/readiness_issues.py`):

- The flagged words are a link (the same action as "Show in chapter").
- "Each place: 1 2 3 …" for a finding repeated in several places, one link
  each, up to `MAX_PLACES` (12). `_places_html`.
- A finding with no exact place still links when it quotes the chapter
  shown; the chapter is searched for the quote. `_showable`. Findings on the
  Whole manuscript row with no chapter get no link.
- "What it looks for: …" under a check at "needs work" or "a problem", from
  the check's `description`.

**Its own window** (new `src/ui/critique_window.py`):

- `PopOut` wraps the Critique's contents in one body widget and moves that
  between the tab and a `CritiqueWindow`. `grader_widget` stays the tab's
  page, so every `setCurrentWidget(self.grader_widget)` still works.
- **Entries:** "⧉ Own window" on the scorecard's top row (it becomes
  "⧉ Back to the tab"), and "Critique in Its Own Window" in the Critique
  menu (`add_to_menu`).
- **First time:** the main window goes to the left of the screen and the
  Critique to the right (`SHARE`, 42%). After that it opens where it was
  left (`critique_window/geometry` in settings).
- **While out:** the main window shows Write; a link in the Critique brings
  the main window forward with the passage selected; the Critique tab shows
  "Show it · Bring it back here", and choosing the tab raises the window.
- **Back:** close the window, the button, or the menu entry.
- **Call lines in `main_window.py`** (rule 14): `critique_window.install(self)`
  after the tabs are added; `add_to_menu` twice in `_populate_review_menu`.

**Tests:** `tests/test_critique_window.py` (5).

**Left out, and for later pieces:**

- **Rule 13:** the button adds one to the Critique tab's count and the menu
  entry one to the Critique menu. It replaces nothing. Worth the space
  because side by side was not possible in one step before; the writer can
  ask for the button to go, keeping the menu entry.
- **Two ways to detach now exist:** this one, and Multi-Window Mode
  (right-click a tab → Create New Window). [D.4](49-D.4-fewer-top-level-tabs.md)
  makes them one mechanism.
- **Not reopened at start-up.** Whether it was out is recorded
  (`critique_window/out`) but nothing reads it yet.
- **Reports from the detailed critique** do not yet link quotes to the text;
  [D.5](49b-D.5-analyses-run-as-critique-reports.md) notes it.
