No new screen in this phase except one small form. Its output is a report the
scorecard will display.

## The piece

- **Adds checks:** continuity conflicts, order of events, and whether dialogue
  fits each character's way of speaking.
- **Uses:** `src/ai/continuity_analysis.py`, `src/ai/event_order.py`,
  `dialogue_craft.dialogue_issues` and `line_fit`.
- **Tiers:** confirm in 0.1 which of these work without a model. A check that
  cannot run without one reports "needs a model" and is left out of the
  readiness figure in the no-AI tier.
- **Done when:** the checks appear and the no-AI report is honest about what it
  could not check.
