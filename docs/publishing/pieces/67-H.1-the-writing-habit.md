## Why

On 24 November 2026 the writer decided that **the writing habit is in scope
and important** (open decision 2). Writing apps the app is measured against
help a writer keep going:

- Scrivener has session and project targets with a deadline.
- Dabble has daily goals and a calendar of the days written.
- Ulysses has goals per sheet and per project.

Community features come later, on a web page of their own, after the first
working product. They are not part of this piece.

## What exists already (checked 24 November 2026)

- **Words written today.** `companion_state.record_session` stores, per
  session, how many words a chapter grew (`"sessions"` in
  `<stem>.companion_state.json`). `companion_state.words_today` sums today's
  sessions.
- **A daily goal that cannot be set.** The Companion reads `daily_word_goal`
  from `creativeos_config` (`companion_panel._daily_goal`), and "Where you
  left off" then shows "Today: N of GOAL words". **Nothing in the app sets
  it**, so in practice there is no goal.
- **What it misses:**
  - Only growth counts (`max(0, words_now − words_at_open)`). A day spent
    cutting or revising counts as zero, so a good editing day looks like a
    day off.
  - The history is trimmed after 400 sessions, so older days are lost.
  - It is kept per project. A writer working on two books sees two
    separate totals, never the day as a whole.

## The piece

**The writer gets:**

1. **A goal, set where it is shown.**
   - "Where you left off" in the Companion gets a quiet link, "Set a daily
     goal…", only while no goal is set.
   - **A daily goal and a weekly goal,** either or both (words per day,
     words per week). Progress toward each shows separately.
   - The same goal is in Settings ▸ Features ▸ Writing habit.
   - Off until set. With no goal, nothing new appears except that one link.
2. **A record of each day.** For each date it keeps:
   - words added;
   - words cut;
   - minutes of active writing (typing, with idle time over a few minutes
     not counted);
   - which projects and chapters were worked on.

   **A writing day is a calendar day** (midnight to midnight, local time)
   **on which the writer wrote**: any words added, cut or revised in a
   chapter. Cutting and revising are writing and keep the streak going.
   Meeting a goal is not needed; the goals are measured separately.
   **One record for all projects:** the streak, the counts and the goals
   are the writer's, shared across every project.
3. **A streak,** the main measure: "N days in a row", counting
   consecutive calendar days with writing. A day with no writing ends it.
   Beside it, "N days this month" and the longest streak so far, so a
   broken streak still leaves a record of the work.
4. **One small view, no new tab:**
   - In the Companion's "Where you left off": "Today: 640 of 1,000 words ·
     this week 3,200 of 5,000 · 5 days in a row".
   - A link opens a small window with the last 12 weeks as a row of day
     squares (shaded by words), this week's total, and the best week.
   - Clicking a day lists what was worked on. Nothing else.
5. **A quiet note when a goal is met:** "Daily goal met: 1,000 words" (or
   the weekly one) in the status bar, through `notify.done` (D.9). No box
   and no sound.
6. **A gentle nudge to move on.**
   - **When:** for a novel or novella, the writer's recent writing days are
     all cutting and revising, with almost no new words. For example, over
     the last 7 writing days the manuscript grew by less than 200 words in
     all, and the draft is not finished.
   - **How:** the app asks once, when a chapter is opened, never while
     typing: "You've spent your last 7 writing days revising. Keep
     polishing, or move on to the next chapter?" There are three answers:
     - **Go to the next chapter** opens the chapter after the one last
       worked on, or adds one if it is the last.
     - **Keep revising** closes the prompt. It asks again after 7 more such
       days, not before.
     - **Stop these nudges** turns the nudge off for good. It is the same
       switch as Settings ▸ Features ▸ Writing habit ▸ "Nudge me to move on
       when I've only been revising", which can turn it back on.
   - **Not for every revising day:**
     - Not once the writer marks the manuscript as being edited (a chapter
       stage past drafting, 3.1), because revising is then the job.
     - Not for a short story.
     - Not more than once a week.

**The writer never gets:** guilt messages, reminders while typing, or the
app nagging when a day is missed. The one prompt is the nudge above, which
the writer can turn off. No feature is removed.

## Where the record is kept

- **The habit belongs to the writer, not to a project.** The day record goes
  in `~/.creativeos/writing_habit.json`, with each day split by project (the
  project's name and file path) so either view is possible.
- **It is written only by the app's own session-recording path**, so no
  chapter file changes. It is never trimmed: a day is a few dozen bytes, so
  ten years is well under a megabyte.
- **Days from before this piece are not lost.** On first run, the existing
  `"sessions"` of each project that is opened are folded into the record
  (growth only, since that is all they hold). The old key is left in place
  and still written, so an older app still shows "words today" (rule 11).
- **The goals** are stored in `creativeos_config`: `daily_word_goal` (the
  key the Companion already reads) and a new `weekly_word_goal`. An existing install has no goal, so nothing changes until
  the writer sets one.

## Build notes

_Names were checked against the code on 24 November 2026; if one has moved, find it with a search and say so in Outcome._

- **New modules:**
  - `src/data/writing_habit.py`: the day record, folding in old sessions,
    writing days and the streak. No Qt.
  - `src/ui/habit_view.py`: the 12-week window, the goal dialog and the
    Settings group.
  - Files over 3,000 lines get call lines only (rule 14).
- **Counting:**
  - Words added and cut come from the editor's text at open and at each
    save or chapter switch, the same points `record_session` already uses.
    Compare word lists (a diff), not just the totals, so a cut paragraph
    and a new one don't cancel out.
  - Pasting a large block (over 300 words at once) or importing from Word
    counts as moved text, not written words, and is listed separately.
  - Minutes: keystrokes in the editor, in one-minute buckets; a bucket
    counts if it has any typing.
- **The streak:** a pure function of the day record, so it can be tested
  on fixed dates. Days are calendar days in the writer's local time;
  writing after midnight counts on the new day. The week starts on
  Monday.
- **The nudge:** a pure function `revising_only(days, project)` in
  `writing_habit.py` decides whether to ask, and returns the numbers for
  the wording.
  - "Novel or novella": the project's kind (`publishing_goal`), and a
    novella sub-project counts.
  - "Draft not finished": no chapter's stage is past drafting.
  - The answer is stored in `creativeos_config` (`habit_nudge`: on or off,
    and the date it last asked).
  - The prompt is a `QMessageBox.question` with the three buttons, because
    it asks for a decision (D.9 keeps boxes for decisions). It is shown
    only when a chapter is opened.
- **UI:** `ui_kit` parts and `PALETTE` only (D.8). The day squares use
  shades of `primary`.
- **Rule 13:**
  - Adds one link (only while no goal is set), one link to the 12-week
    view, and one Settings group.
  - Run the harness before and after. A count that rises goes to checklist
    section 3.
- **Tests:**
  - A day with any writing counts toward the streak, whether or not a goal
    was met; a day of cutting counts too.
  - A calendar day with no writing ends the streak; writing at 00:30
    counts on the new day.
  - The daily and weekly goals are measured separately; the week starts on
    Monday.
  - The streak and counts are shared: writing in two projects on one day
    counts once for the streak, with the words of both.
  - The nudge asks after 7 revising-only writing days on an unfinished
    novel, not on a short story, and not once a chapter is past drafting.
    "Keep revising" waits 7 more days; "Stop these nudges" turns it off and
    the Settings switch turns it back on.
  - A large paste is not counted as written.
  - Old sessions fold in once and the old key is still written.
  - With no goal, the Companion shows exactly what it did before, plus the
    one link.
  - A project and settings file from before this piece open unchanged.

**Where it is stored:**

- `~/.creativeos/writing_habit.json`, written only through `safe_store`
  (temporary file, then swapped in; an unreadable file is set aside, never
  saved over). One record for the writer: `days` → date → project key (the
  project file's path, or `unsaved:<name>`) → name, path, added, cut,
  moved, minutes, and chapters (id → title, added, cut, moved). `folded`
  marks the projects whose old sessions are in; `last` keeps the chapter
  last worked on per project (for "Go to the next chapter"). Never trimmed;
  unknown keys are kept.
- `creativeos_config`: `daily_word_goal` (the key the Companion already
  read), new `weekly_word_goal`, and new `habit_nudge` (`{"on": …,
  "last_asked": "YYYY-MM-DD"}`). A key is written only when its value
  changes, so opening and saving Settings with no goal adds nothing. An
  older app ignores the new keys; it still reads `daily_word_goal` and
  shows "Today: N of GOAL words" from its own sessions.
- Nothing in a project file, chapter file or `<project>.companion_state.json`
  changed. `companion_state.record_session` is untouched and still writes
  `"sessions"` (and still trims them at 400, as before; the new record
  keeps everything).

**How old sessions were folded in:** the first time a chapter of a project
is opened, that project's companion `"sessions"` (one per chapter visit:
date and growth) are added to the record as words added on their dates,
with chapter titles looked up in the project. The project is then marked in
`folded` and never folded again, so nothing is counted twice; the sessions
themselves are left as they are. They hold growth only, so days of pure
cutting before H.1 are not in the record (they were never stored).

**How words are counted** (`HabitTracker`, created by the Companion's
controller for each chapter editor):

- The text at open is the starting point. At each save or chapter switch
  (`chapter_saved`, the point `record_session` already used), the words
  added and cut since the last save come from a diff of the word lists
  (`writing_habit.word_changes`: common start and end trimmed, then
  `difflib`; a word-count difference for enormous rewrites), so a cut
  paragraph and a new one don't cancel out. The starting point then moves
  on, so each save counts only what changed since the one before.
- A single change of more than 300 words (a paste, an import from Word, an
  undo that restores a cut) is counted as moved, not written. It is caught
  from the document's `contentsChange`; format-only changes from the
  highlighter report equal removed and added lengths and are skipped.
- Minutes: key presses with text (and Backspace, Delete, Return) mark
  one-minute buckets; a minute counts once even across saves.
- A save whose minutes span midnight shares its words out by the minutes
  typed on each day, so writing after midnight counts on the new day.
- A writing day is any calendar day with words added, cut or moved.
